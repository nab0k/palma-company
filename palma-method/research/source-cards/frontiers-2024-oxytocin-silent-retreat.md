# Changes in peripheral oxytocin and vasopressin during a silent month-long Insight meditation retreat

## Status

**Checked, not verified.** Real, peer-reviewed, directly on-topic. The
finding is counterintuitive (oxytocin decreased, not increased) - do not
let this get simplified into a generic "silence raises bonding hormones"
line in the manuscript. That would misrepresent the actual study.
