# Abercrombie & Fitch — audience-narrowing case

## Priority given to primary text / official data

Company's own FY2023 earnings disclosure (primary), cross-checked against independent
retail-press coverage of the multi-year strategic shift.

## Specific fragments supporting the claim

16% overall revenue growth, 27% brand-specific growth, 15-year operating-margin high —
company-reported figures, consistently repeated in independent coverage.

## Why this source deserves a place in the book

It gives Chapter 18 a quantified, recent business parallel for exactly the design
decision the chapter's TAK.Shtab material describes: narrowing rather than trying to
serve everyone.

## What is original in it

Not original theory — a real, quantified business case.

## Limitations / competing interpretation

Single-company case; broader sector tailwinds in the period are a possible partial
confound not fully isolated by the company's own figures.

## Permissible level of claim

"A&F narrowed its audience starting in 2017 and reported strong FY2023 growth
alongside that strategy" — supportable, sourced to the company's own disclosures.
"The narrowing strategy alone caused the growth" — overclaims causal isolation the
data doesn't cleanly support; the manuscript's phrasing ("рост пришёл не от того, что
бренд стал шире... он пришёл от того, что бренд стал точнее") stays close to the
company's own account without asserting an independently isolated causal proof.

## Forbidden overgeneralization

Do not present this as proof that narrowing always outperforms broadening in retail —
one case, one company, one period.
