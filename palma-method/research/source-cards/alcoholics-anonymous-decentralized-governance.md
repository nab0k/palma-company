# Alcoholics Anonymous: Decentralized, Founder-Independent Governance Structure

## Status

**Checked, not verified to primary-study standard.** Governance structure
(Twelve Traditions, rotating roles, home-group voting) is well corroborated
across AA's own published materials and independent coverage. This card is
scoped strictly to governance/continuation mechanism - explicitly not a
clinical-effectiveness claim, which is a separate and more contested
literature this project does not draw on.

## Why this card was created

TASK_CLAUDE_0031's international-case research identified AA as an unusually
sharp contrastive pair to the Goenka Vipassana system already anchoring
Chapter 1: both are founder-independent, old-timer-helps-newcomer systems
that scaled past their founders, but by structurally opposite mechanisms
(radical decentralization vs. content standardization).
