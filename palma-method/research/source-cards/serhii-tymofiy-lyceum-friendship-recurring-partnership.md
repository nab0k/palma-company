## Two counterfactuals worth preserving verbatim in any future draft

1. If contact had lapsed 4 years right after the lyceum, would Timofey
   have been hired in 2007? Author's answer: probably not.
2. If contact had continued but no further joint work ever happened,
   would the tie be as close today? Author's answer: probably not either.

Both point the same direction: neither shared past nor sustained contact
alone explains the tie's persistence - some combination with recurring
joint practice appears necessary. Treat as the author's own hypothesis,
not a finding.
