# CrossFit Affiliate Licensing Model

## Status

**Checked, not verified to primary-study standard.** Affiliate counts, fee
structure, and the 2020s closure trend are corroborated across multiple
independent business-press sources, but no single peer-reviewed primary
study of the model itself has been located. Usable as an illustration, not
as proof of a causal mechanism.

## Why this card was created

TASK_CLAUDE_0031's international-case research identified this as an
unusually strong candidate specifically because the book's own central
lived case (TCF) is itself structurally a CrossFit-style gym, and because it
offers a genuine third structural option (cheap decentralized enablement)
against the two mechanisms already active in the manuscript's Chapter 8
(Harley-Davidson's centralized build, Google+'s absence of infrastructure).
