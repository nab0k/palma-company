## What this source contributes

The interview supplies a high-regularity perspective that the earlier five-member
synthesis did not contain. It is especially useful because the participant describes
multiple direct ties while also saying that convenience, not the booking list, governs
his normal schedule. This prevents regular co-attendance from being treated as proof
of deliberate coordination.

The source also identifies small, observable interaction windows: pairing during
strength work and several minutes before or after class. The participant describes
the founder as a major source of tone, introductions, and activity, but the examples
include participant-to-participant ties at different levels of independence.

## Required checks before manuscript use

1. Confirm the consent, naming, quotation, and off-record boundaries.
2. Check any proposed quotation against the audio.
3. Genericize or separately clear every third party.
4. Separate participant observation from hearsay about other members.
5. Compare self-reported scheduling with private attendance data only in a private
   analysis; do not expose individual attendance records.
