# Author story intake — Serhii at TCF

## Recoverable account

Serhii did not begin attending TCF because he wanted to join a community. He
wanted a reliable structure that did not require him to organize a social life
before entering it. With repetition, familiar names in the booking app began
to influence which classes he chose. He started arriving early, sometimes
working from the space, learned to use the coffee machine, and occasionally
made coffee for other people. At some point the relationship changed: he no
longer experienced himself only as a customer.

Serhii has also stated that he met his partner at TCF. That detail is recorded
only as a private verification lead. It must not enter the manuscript without
separate approval from both recognizable people.

## Why the story may matter

The story supplies a first-person transition that the TCF founder interviews
cannot provide: service user, repeat participant, familiar face, small
contributor. It can illustrate that belonging may emerge from low-stakes
repetition and small acts of usefulness rather than from an explicit decision
to “join a community.”

## Required author expansion

Before manuscript use, obtain Serhii's own wording for:

1. the first day and what he was looking for;
2. the first familiar name that affected a booking decision;
3. the first time he stayed without needing to train;
4. the coffee-machine scene and who was present;
5. the moment he realized he was no longer only a customer;
6. what he gave, received, and still found difficult;
7. which personal details may be published.
