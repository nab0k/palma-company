# TCF former-member couple interview — 16 August 2026

## Source and use status

- Private source: full 28:26 machine transcript in Notion and original audio
  outside GitHub.
- Speaker check, quotation check, and publication consent remain open.
- The two participants and every third party remain unnamed in this derivative.

## What this source contributes

This is both a migration-entry account and a continuation case. It shows why a
nearby recurring practice can matter to someone who has just arrived in a country,
but it also records how one relationship moved into direct routes involving running,
housing, work preparation, and continued contact after relocation.

The source is unusually vulnerable to interviewer influence because Serhii is part
of the main sequence. It should therefore be used as a traceable candidate case and
checked with the participants, not presented as independent corroboration.

## Required checks before manuscript use

1. Separate the three speakers against the audio.
2. Ask each participant to confirm the chronology independently.
3. Obtain consent for paraphrase, quotations, and identifiable details.
4. Distinguish what began at TCF from what later depended on friendship, language,
   neighborhood proximity, or individual initiative.
