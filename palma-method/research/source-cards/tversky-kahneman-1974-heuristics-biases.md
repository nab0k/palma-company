# Judgment under Uncertainty: Heuristics and Biases

## Editorial use

Use only to name availability, representativeness, and anchoring when the
mechanism is actually present. Do not turn the chapter into a catalogue of
biases or attribute unrelated concepts to Kahneman alone.
