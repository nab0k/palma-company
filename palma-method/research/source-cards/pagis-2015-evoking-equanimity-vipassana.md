## Why this is the right source for Serhii's Vipassana case

It is the closest thing to a direct academic study of exactly what Serhii
described: strangers who never speak, following one shared schedule, ending
a retreat with a strong felt sense of connection to the group. Pagis gives
the book real vocabulary for this - "silent interaction ritual," a
"sociality of non-engagement" - instead of forcing the case to borrow
loosely from theories built around ordinary conversation-based bonding.
