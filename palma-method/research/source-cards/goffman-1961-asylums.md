# Asylums: Essays on the Social Situation of Mental Patients and Other Inmates

## Status

**Checked, not verified.** Classic, correctly attributed. Not yet read
directly.

## Why this card exists

Not to support a claim, but to guard against a specific overclaim already
flagged in the private working draft for Serhii's Vipassana case (SC-054):
the retreat's intense, imposed-feeling schedule must never be described
using Goffman's "total institution" framing without an explicit,
foregrounded qualification that the retreat was voluntary, time-limited,
and freely exitable - the exact opposite of what makes a total institution
what it is.
