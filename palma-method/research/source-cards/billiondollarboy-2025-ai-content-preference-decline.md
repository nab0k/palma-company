# Muse/Billion Dollar Boy AI-content preference decline, 2023 vs. 2025

## Status

**Discovery, weakest card of this research pass.** Contradicted by a
separate, comparably-sized Capgemini survey showing the opposite direction
(73% trust AI content). Do not cite the 60%-to-26% figure in the manuscript
without naming this contradiction, or better, without first reading both
primary reports and reconciling the methodological difference.
