## What the account contributes

This conversation moves from general purpose to observable social mechanics.
The founder describes a welcome sequence in which each entrant is noticed,
first-time visitors are made comfortable, and someone later checks how the
experience went. He also describes an informal high-five that spread from one
coach to other coaches and members. If participants confirm it, this can show
how a repeated gesture becomes a ritual without a formal campaign.

Coffee appears as infrastructure rather than decoration. Members can remain
before or after training, and people can visit without the workout being the
only reason to stay. The booking app supplies another small piece of
infrastructure: familiar names are visible before a class, and stable time
groups can form around recurring schedules.

The interview offers several signs of member-to-member value—friendships,
activities outside the gym, possible work connections, and shared viewing of
football—but most examples are still founder-reported. It also exposes an
important rival interpretation: familiar names can make attendance easier for
insiders while increasing pressure or exclusion for newcomers.
