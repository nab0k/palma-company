# The Ritual Process: Structure and Anti-Structure

## Status

**Checked, not verified.** A real, foundational, widely-cited monograph -
low risk of misattribution since "communitas" and "liminality" are
uncontroversially Turner's terms. Not yet read directly by this project.

## Why this is the right source for Serhii's Vipassana case

It supplies the two things a Vipassana chapter needs most: a name for the
phenomenon Serhii felt (communitas, produced through liminality), and a
built-in caution against overselling it - Turner's own theory says this kind
of bond is structurally temporary unless the group deliberately re-embeds it
in lasting structure. That caution keeps the book from turning a beautiful
personal story into an implied promise ("send your team on a retreat and
they'll bond like this too") that the theory itself would contradict.
