# TCF founder interview continuation — 5 August 2026

## Source and use status

This card closes the registration gap between the two earlier founder cards and
the private 5 August continuation. It does not clear third-party stories for use.

## What this source contributes

The continuation moves the founder-side case from general atmosphere to candidate
mechanisms: intentional introductions, recurring contact, newcomer facilitation,
and relationships that may or may not become independent of the gym. Equally
important, the founder acknowledges incomplete visibility into the network.

## Required checks before manuscript use

1. Verify every participant-side episode with the people involved.
2. Check quotations and names against audio.
3. Retain negative and boundary cases rather than selecting only successful ties.
4. Separate intentional founder action from later participant action.
