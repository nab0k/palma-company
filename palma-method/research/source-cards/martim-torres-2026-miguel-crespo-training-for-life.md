# O teu treino está a preparar-te para a vida? — Miguel Crespo

## Permissible use

- Evidence of Miguel's stated rationale and reported operating practice.
- Concrete prompts for observation and participant interviews.
- Comparison with SC-043 and SC-044 as stability in the founder's account, not
  independent corroboration of outcomes.

## Prohibited inference

- Do not infer that these practices caused retention, health, trust, friendship,
  or community.
- Do not count the interview as another member account.
- Do not quote the auto-caption wording without checking the video audio.

## Manuscript use

Chapter 5 uses three bounded points: shared activity adapted through weights,
angles, and variants; welcoming a late participant instead of publicly punishing
them; and recommending three weekly sessions instead of an unlimited plan when
the founder considers the latter unsustainable. The passage labels the source as
the founder's account and explicitly reserves outcome testing for attendance data
and participant interviews.
