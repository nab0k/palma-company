**Checked, not verified.** One of the most famous studies in social

## Why this matters for SC-054

Gives the "sea guy" scene its sharpest framing line: if people will invent
motives and personalities for two triangles and a circle, of course a room
full of silent strangers will do the same, and more richly, for each other.
