  Traditional hotel brands built trust institutionally - through decades of standardized experience
  and corporate accountability (the "Marriott" or "Hilton" name itself as the trust signal). Only
  roughly 10-15% of hotel guests complete a post-stay review. Airbnb instead built trust
  relationally, host-by-host: 70-75% of Airbnb hosts and guests review each other after a stay, and
  guests report rating Airbnb stays more highly than hotel stays, citing personal interaction and
  local authenticity as factors hotels' institutional model doesn't supply.

  purchase (a place to stay), one built on brand/institutional reputation, one built on accumulated
  peer-to-peer relational signals - and the relational system produces both far higher engagement
  (review completion) and, per guest reports, higher satisfaction.
