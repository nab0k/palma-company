sensitive material in this card, independent of any privacy rule about

someone very, very important in my life. And I had people from TCF
helping me with the grief, helping me help the family of this friend...

in this excerpt. Given this project's existing, separately-confirmed
knowledge of a real death connected to the TCF community (documented
elsewhere in this project's working material, not in this card), this
disclosure should be treated as likely referring to the same loss -
**but that connection is drawn here only as a caution, not as a claim to
publish.** Regardless of whether it is the same loss: do not use this
material in any form, quoted or paraphrased, without a separate,
explicit conversation with Luciana about what - if anything - she would
be comfortable having described. She volunteered this in the context of
answering a question about reciprocity and help received, not as
something offered for a book about a gym's social dynamics; using it
without that separate check would be a real breach of the spirit in
which it was shared, whatever the letter of any consent form might

Miguel and Margarida are named directly in the transcript throughout;

## What the account contributes

Luciana is the most enthusiastic and best-connected of the four TCF
member interviews now on record, and precisely because of that, her one
critical observation carries unusual weight: measured against a CrossFit
community she belonged to in Brazil, she says TCF members convert to
outside-the-gym friends less often, and the Brazilian gym ran more
organized events and workshops beyond training itself. This is the first
same-person, cross-context comparison this project has - not a single
member's disposition, but one person's side-by-side read of two real
communities on the same dimension.

Her relationship history with Miguel is a clean, dated instance of
dormant-tie reactivation: a real prior acquaintance (2019, a different
gym, no real connection made) that lay unused for years until a new
shared context - TCF's opening - reactivated it. This sits naturally
alongside the project's existing dormant-ties literature (SC-090, SC-091).

Her arrival story adds a second ordinary-member illustration (after
Anya, SC-097) of Chapter 3's reputation-precedes-access pattern: she
recognized Miguel's face in an Instagram ad before ever speaking to him
at TCF, and that recognition - not the ad's content - is what moved her
from curiosity to same-day subscription.
