## Honest gap this card documents

A dedicated search for peer-reviewed research specifically on "eating style
reveals personality" found only popular-psychology listicles, not real
studies - that specific claim should not be cited as scientific in the
manuscript. This paper's broader "behavioral residue" finding is the
closest legitimate anchor: it establishes the general principle (indirect,
non-verbal behavioral traces carry real signal) without overclaiming the
specific, unstudied case of eating behavior.
