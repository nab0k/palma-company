# Author story intake — career chronology and unseparated case corpus

## Purpose

This card is a discovery map for author material that was preserved in a
private chronological handoff but has not yet earned separate case cards. It
does not supersede SC-046–SC-049, SC-054, SC-074, SC-082, SC-085, or SC-089.
It prevents a future editor from concluding that these stories do not exist
merely because they are absent from the current manuscript or source index.
