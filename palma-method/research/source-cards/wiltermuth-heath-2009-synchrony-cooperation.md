# Synchrony and Cooperation

## Status

**Checked, not verified.** Real, citable, well-known paper in this
literature. Not yet read directly; replication status of the specific

## Why this is a useful secondary source for Serhii's Vipassana case

It separates out one mechanism the Pagis and Turner sources don't isolate on
their own: the retreat's identical, shared physical schedule (not just its
silence or its liminal framing) may itself be doing real bonding work. Worth
asking Serhii directly whether the schedule's rigid synchrony felt like part
of the bonding experience, or whether it was invisible to him at the time.
