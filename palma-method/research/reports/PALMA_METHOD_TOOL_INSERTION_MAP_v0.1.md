## Four-stage TAK.Shtab walkthrough boundary

The manuscript now carries TAK.Shtab through Diagnose, Design, Launch, and Transfer
as a verified, public-safe walkthrough assembled from already published manuscript
facts and SC-110's dated public corroboration. It says explicitly that:

- the tools were not applied prospectively in 2022;
- the reconstruction is retrospective and hypothesis-generating;
- the convoy is an illustration of behavior under an unplanned event, not a Method
  experiment;
- 2024 public features are consistent with institutionalization but no formal
  Handoff Test occurred;
- a future Handoff Test would require a preselected function, natural observation
  window, bounded founder availability, and recorded continuation/stoppage.

## Founder/operator worked example

The conclusion also provides a public-safe generic operator example: a client
relationship concentrated in one manager. It walks through one dyadic/network map,
one second-route intervention, advance success/failure signals, behavior observed at
Launch, and a later function-specific Handoff Test. It introduces no personal or
client fact and makes no claim that the example already occurred.
