Status: draft for editorial review (Codex / Serhii)
Source: `palma-method/research/chapter-packets/PALMA_METHOD_MANUSCRIPT_v0.2.md`,
Chapters 17-21 and the conclusion only. **Nothing was deleted or edited in
the manuscript to produce this map.** Every block below keeps its exact
manuscript origin and gets an exact proposed destination; a block proposed
for the appendix or scientific note is understood to be *duplicated there*,
not removed from the chapter, unless the Chief Editor separately decides to
shorten the chapter - a decision this task does not make.

## Four categories

1. **Stay** - remains in the main narrative unchanged.
2. **Shorten** - the idea stays in the main narrative, but its full technical
   detail moves elsewhere; the chapter keeps a brief, readable version.
3. **Appendix** - full operational detail belongs in
   `PALMA_METHOD_FIELD_GUIDE_APPENDIX_v0.1.md`.
4. **Scientific note** - belongs in
   `PALMA_METHOD_EVIDENCE_AND_SCIENTIFIC_STATUS_v0.1.md`.

## Summary of appendix consolidation

The single largest structural finding of this map: **the TAK.Shtab walkthrough
and the generic founder/client example each currently exist in more than one
partial form across Chapters 17, 18, 21, and the conclusion.** Rather than
recommending four small trims in four places, this map recommends one
consolidation: a single, complete TAK.Shtab walkthrough and a single,
complete generic example, both told once and in full in the appendix
(§12), with each chapter keeping only a short, in-context pointer to it.
This is a structural recommendation for the Chief Editor's decision, not an
instruction - the manuscript itself is unchanged by this task either way.
