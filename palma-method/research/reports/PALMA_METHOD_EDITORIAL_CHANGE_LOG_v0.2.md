v0.2 remains unchanged. Chapters 1–16 remain unchanged except for one sentence at
the end of the introduction that gives the reader an early, plain-language preview
of the four movements of the Method.

## Editorial mode

`nabok-editor` REPAIR mode was used for the AI-heavy Part IV synchronization prose.
The personal openings, facts, uncertainty, humor, and existing authorial sentences
were protected. The repair relies mainly on deletion, relocation, evidence
qualification, and short local substitutions rather than a wholesale imitation of
the author's voice.

## Changes by section

- Added a one-sentence reader contract: understand the problem, design a small

- Kept the book's question and Relationship Capital definitions unchanged.

### Chapter 17 — Diagnose
