# Manuscript v0.10 editorial reconciliation ledger

## Executive result

This ledger replaces the convenient statement that the old set of more than
fifty comments was simply “implemented.” The original Notion audit accounted for
45 unresolved discussions: 42 founder comments and three editorial notes. Those
threads, earlier manuscript comments, later founder instructions, research gates,
and case decisions were normalized into the accepted 77-item v0.8 register.

Against the actual text of v0.10, the 77 items now divide as follows:

| Current status | Count | Meaning |
|---|---:|---|
| `closed_in_v0.10` | 42 | The requested change is visibly present and no special decision remains. |
| `founder_line_review` | 24 | The requested material is present, but its wording, weight, or whole-book balance awaits Serhii's read. |
| `partial` | 8 | A material requested component is still absent or the whole-book pass is incomplete. |
| `open_author_decision` | 1 | The Kichkine teacher scene still requires exact public wording or permanent omission. |
| `superseded` | 2 | A later founder instruction changed the earlier direction; the replacement is present. |
| **Total** | **77** | Every V08 item is accounted for exactly once. |

The manuscript is therefore substantially rebuilt, but the historical editorial
register is not yet closed. “Text exists” and “founder accepted the text” are kept
separate here.

## Status rules

- `closed_in_v0.10` means the direction can be located in the current manuscript.
- `founder_line_review` is not a research blocker. It means Serhii explicitly
  asked to read or polish the resulting prose before acceptance.
- `partial` names the missing component instead of treating a cautious omission
  as full implementation.
- `superseded` preserves the old instruction and records the later replacement.
