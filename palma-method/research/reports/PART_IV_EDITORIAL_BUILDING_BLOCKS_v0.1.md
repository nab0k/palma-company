a direct connection to the book's own vocabulary, which it currently lacks.

**Evidence / source cards supporting each block**: convoy scene (at least four
missions, Bucha as a destination, the landmine detonation, the video of destroyed

## Chapter 20 — currently "Совет" / Advise (510 words)

**Function as currently written**: distinguish giving advice from deciding for
someone, and show why staying in the process-consultant role is harder than it
sounds, especially with people you love.

**Architecture decision needed (not made here)**: per the tool spec, Advise/
Facilitate is a cross-cutting principle, not this book's fifth sequential stage.
Three ways to signal this in the chapter, none chosen:

1. **Keep the chapter, reframe its opening/closing.** Retitle or subtitle it as a
   principle interlude rather than "stage 4 of 5," add one sentence early and one
   late explicitly naming it as a stance that applies throughout Diagnose, Design,
   Launch, and Transfer, not a stage after Launch. Lowest editorial cost; keeps the
   Bombom material and the Schein/Brehm theory intact as-is.
2. **Distribute the material.** Break the chapter's content into short "Advise/
   Facilitate" call-outs inserted into each of the other four chapters, and remove
   it as a standalone chapter. Highest editorial cost; loses the clean, isolated
   illustration the Bombom case currently provides (the author had no authority to
   decide anything there, which is precisely what makes the principle visible).
3. **Keep it as a standalone chapter with its current title and position**, and rely
   on front-matter (the introduction's method map, per the KDP positioning research)
   to tell the reader Advise is a cross-cutting principle rather than expecting the
   chapter itself to carry that framing. Lowest content risk; leaves a structural
   inconsistency between what the introduction promises (four stages) and what the
   chapter sequence shows (five named chapters) unless the introduction is very
   explicit about the distinction.

**Keep regardless of which option is chosen**: the Bombom closure scene; the
Schein expert-vs-process distinction and Humble Inquiry material; the Brehm
reactance material; the cold-outreach-vs-existing-relationship contrast (new to
this chapter, not reused elsewhere); the Schein/DEC detail, which already complies
with the explicit instruction not to use DEC's fate as proof of the approach's
effectiveness or ineffectiveness.

**Cut**: nothing identified — the chapter is Part IV's thinnest (510 words) and
the editorial need is development, not trimming. The architecture audit
(TASK_CLAUDE_0018) flags that this is the sixth chapter to use the Bombom case,
and recommends considering whether the chapter needs a second, non-Bombom
illustration to stand on its own — this is a content decision, not addressed
further here per this task's prohibition on adding new research without necessity.

**Tool to insert**: none of the five main tools maps cleanly onto this chapter as a
standalone artifact, which is itself evidence for the cross-cutting reading (option
1/2 above) rather than a numbered-stage reading. If the chapter stays standalone
(option 3), consider naming the Relationship Capital Map dimension it's actually
testing (trust) explicitly, to keep it connected to the tool vocabulary.

**Evidence / source cards supporting each block**: Bombom closure, cold outreach —
`bombom-founder-interview-2026-08-03.md` (SC-045, including its 2026-08-08

**Exact TAK.Shtab facts usable**: none — this chapter does not currently draw on

## Conclusion — building blocks only, no prose

The manuscript currently has no separate conclusion chapter (Chapter 21 closes Part
IV, not the book). Per the KDP positioning research supplied by the author, the
introduction's question is:

> **Почему способность связаться с человеком не равна его готовности ответить,
> помочь или действовать вместе — и что создаёт эту готовность?**

**What a conclusion needs to do, stated as building blocks:**

1. **Answer the introduction's question directly, in one line**, using the chain
   already implicit across the book: reach → relationship conditions (trust,
   reciprocity, access, tie strength, network structure) → reciprocal response
   (Palma Moment) → infrastructure (routes, roles, rhythms, channels, memory) →
   mobilizable cooperation → transfer. This is the book's own defensible synthesis,
   not a claim to a new scientific law (see the KDP research's "what cannot be
   claimed" list — avoid any phrasing implying discovery of a new causal mechanism).
2. **Close without implying every organization needs a community.** Bombom must
   stay explicitly not-inferior to TCF — this discipline is already maintained
   throughout the book (Chapter 16 states it directly) and should not be lost in the
   conclusion's own rhetorical push toward a clean ending.
3. **Do not present relationship-building as automatically beneficial.** Chapter
   10's dark-side material (Portes, Bourdieu, Uzzi) and Chapter 20's reactance
   material already establish this; the conclusion should not lose the caution for
   the sake of an uplifting close.
4. **If returning to an opening scene, use one already approved** — Chapter 1's
   hand-clap image (the two-palms metaphor the Method and the word "Palma" are both
   built on) is the most natural candidate, since it is already the book's central
   image and requires no new material.
5. **State the Advise/Facilitate architecture decision with confidence**, once it
   has been made (see Chapter 20 options above) — the conclusion is a natural place
   to state the Method's final shape plainly, since by that point the reader has
   seen all four stages plus the cross-cutting principle in action.
6. **Do not function as a sales pitch** for the Palma Method or for The Collective
   Fitness specifically — TCF's relationship to the Method itself is never asserted
   in the current manuscript (it predates the Method) and this must hold in the
   conclusion too.
7. **Name what the book has NOT proven**, briefly and without undermining its own
   argument: five interviews are not a study of TCF; TAK.Shtab is one case,
   independently corroborated on some points but not others; none of the five tools
   is a validated instrument. This is the same discipline the KDP positioning
   research recommends as an epistemic note, extended to the conclusion's own
   closing tone.

No conclusion prose is drafted here, per this task's explicit instruction.
