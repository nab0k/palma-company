# Manuscript v0.5 edit plan

## Target

Create one append-only successor to
`palma-method/research/chapter-packets/PALMA_METHOD_MANUSCRIPT_v0.4.md` after
founder authorization. v0.4 remains unchanged. The new build must preserve the
book's argument and lived detail while removing unsupported authority, generic
case padding, research-protocol prose, and repeated AI-shaped contrasts.

## Governing editorial outcome

The edited book should make one legible movement:

1. communication becomes relational only when another side produces a meaningful
   counter-action;
2. repeated counter-actions can accumulate value and create participant-to-
   participant routes;
3. those routes may remain dependent on a person, place, or schedule;
4. Palma Method is the proposed four-stage practice for seeing, designing,
   observing, and testing that transition;
5. the components are research-grounded, while Palma's integration remains a set
   of untested propositions rather than a validated technology.

## Edit sequence

The sequence is designed to avoid polishing passages that will later be deleted or
structurally moved.

### Pass A — subtraction and epistemic triage

- Remove Dewey & LeBoeuf, Nvidia/OpenAI, Chief, Abercrombie & Fitch,
  CrowdStrike/Delta, and Zoho.
- Review IBM, Apple no-poach, Fine, Fine/van den Scott, and every remaining imported
  case against AR-024.
- Remove false claims that Palma was applied to TCF or tested through TAK.Shtab,
  Bombom, or client work.
- Remove `scientific note` language, irrelevant dashboard references, follower
  counts, and future-work promises that do not sharpen a present conclusion.
- Correct known factual drift: the headquarters office was not empty; Wodify
  co-attendance has not yet been analyzed; no formal Handoff Test was run.

Output of Pass A: a shorter but epistemically clean structural draft.

### Pass B — authorial and case architecture

- Complete the TCF baseline in Chapter 5, including entry, founders, workout
  structure, recurring presence, coffee, changing role, relationships, Alina/Timur
  sequence, and the limits of the author's view.
- Remove duplicate TCF reintroductions and moved-member repetition; convert later
  appearances into mechanism-specific callbacks.
- Strengthen Anton Nosik's first full introduction once, then keep later callbacks
  brief.
- Repair the ministry/headquarters narrative so strategic communications,
  analytical work, and humanitarian logistics are distinct forms of usefulness.
- Rebuild the Chapter 21 headquarters transformation from verified organizational
  changes rather than symbolic callbacks or social metrics.

Output of Pass B: every major lived case has a beginning, development, consequence,
and declared evidential limit.

### Pass C — theory and scholar integration

For every first appearance apply this compact test:

1. What problem was the scholar studying?
2. What did the work actually examine or compare?
3. What did it find?
4. What does the finding help the reader see here?
5. What does it not establish?

Use it for the named comments in the cumulative change map. Do not force all five
answers into a standard paragraph. The bridge may precede the name, and the limit
may appear only where a likely overreach exists. Later citations get a reminder,
not a second literature review.

Output of Pass C: theory advances the narrative and each name has a visible job.

### Pass D — Palma Method and tools

- Give Palma Method one canonical scientific-status statement and one reader
  promise consistent with DECISION_0006.
- Normalize the four stages: Diagnose, Design, Launch, Transfer; Facilitate/Advise
  remains cross-cutting.
- Give Palma Moment one definition and two or three verified narrative episodes;
  prevent it from becoming proof of durability or effectiveness.
- Define Response Signals through observable episodes and alternative explanations.
- Rewrite Handoff Test as a bounded proposed procedure; state which retrospective
  evidence is merely suggestive.
- Hold the substantive Infrastructure Canvas rewrite until both commissioned audits
  are available. Then synthesize them into an operational and falsifiable version,
  not a citation-decorated checklist.

Output of Pass D: the reader can distinguish theory, observation, hypothesis, and
procedure, and can understand what the tools ask them to do.

### Pass E — voice and sentence-level repair

- Audit `не X, а Y` and related negative scaffolding.
- Replace editor voice (`важно понимать`, `нужно отметить`, `получается такая
  последовательность`) with argument, scene, or precise transition.
- Remove miniature summaries that merely repeat the preceding paragraph.
- Vary paragraph movement: scene → question, finding → consequence, distinction →
  example, and callback → new implication.
- Preserve the author's humor, concrete objects, abrupt short sentences, and
  qualified first-person judgment. Do not make the prose uniformly academic.
- Audit historical filler (`ещё`, `уже тогда`, calendar-year bibliography) and keep
  chronology only where it changes meaning.

Output of Pass E: a continuous authorial voice without weakening qualifications.

### Pass F — final integrity audit

- Trace factual claims to source cards or canonical verification issues.
- Check all causal verbs, quantities, dates, corporate outcomes, and descriptions of
  research methods.
- Check permissions, anonymization, public safety, and third-party motives.
- Check cross-chapter definitions: Relationship Capital, five dimensions, Palma
  Moment, infrastructure, Canvas fields, Response Signals, and Transfer.
- Check each external case for exact mechanism and necessity.
- Check that every chapter produces a reader gain: distinction, mechanism, question,
  procedure, or consequence.
- Compare introduction promises with conclusion answers.

Output of Pass F: evidence ledger for all changed factual passages and a publication
blocker list containing only unresolved material issues.
