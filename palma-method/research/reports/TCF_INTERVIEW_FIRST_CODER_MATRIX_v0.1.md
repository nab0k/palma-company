privacy-safe derivatives, not independent primary-transcript coding. Values can
change after audio checks or second coding. `U` means relevant but unresolved;
`NA` means not asked, not attributable, or excluded. Neither means absence.

## Descriptive counts from codable member accounts

These are counts of interview accounts, not rates for TCF:

- Consequential participant action (`Q1`): 6 accounts; 1 explicit absence; 1
  unresolved.
- Consequential response (`R1`): 3 accounts; 3 unresolved after an action; 2 not
  applicable/unresolved.
- Outside-gym continuation (`C2` or `C3`): 4 clear accounts; 1 explicit absence;
  3 unresolved.
- Observed post-reduction/exit continuation (`C3`): 2 clear accounts (P2, P7),
  with P7 low-confidence pending independent chronology confirmation.
- Organizer-independent route (`I2`): 6 candidate accounts; none establishes that
  all relevant ties in TCF are organizer-independent.
- Participant-created group channel (`H2`): no participant account in the coded
  derivative establishes one cleanly. The founder reports such groups, making this
  a candidate for participant verification rather than a participant-level finding.

The apparent co-occurrence of `Q1` and stronger continuation is **suggestive only**.
The sample was selected partly for connection, P7's central episode involves the
interviewer, and downstream outcomes were often used during recruitment. The
matrix cannot test a causal sequence.

## What changed relative to the five-case coding

1. The old binary “continued relationship” variable is split into tie formation
   (`T`), class-adjacent contact, outside-gym contact, and observed post-exit
   persistence (`C`). This prevents coffee after class from being treated as the
   same outcome as friendship after relocation.
2. Consequential action (`Q`) is separated from consequential response (`R`). The
   existing material contains several actions whose reception is unknown. These
   are not complete Palma Moments under the stricter two-hand formulation.
3. Organizer warmth, organizer dependence (`D`), and episode-level organizer
   independence (`I`) are separate constructs.
4. P8 exposes a network-attribution issue: a relationship reached through the
   spouse of a TCF participant may be a real network extension without being a
   direct TCF participant tie.
5. P6 weakens the simple schedule-coordination idea: a highly regular and connected
   respondent says convenience usually determines class choice. Co-attendance may
   create opportunity without demonstrating deliberate synchronization.

## Founder context, excluded from counts

Across SC-043, SC-044, and SC-144, F0 describes intentional introductions,
newcomer facilitation, coffee, repeated contact, participant messaging groups, and
relationships reportedly continuing beyond training. The same source also admits
incomplete visibility into the peer network. These statements generate candidate
episodes to verify with participants; they do not fill missing participant cells.

## Provisional conclusions supported now

1. The interviews contain multiple specific peer actions; the material is richer
   than general statements that people “became friends.”
2. Action and response are not equally documented. Only three accounts currently
   contain a clear second move under this codebook.
3. At least two accounts describe relationships persisting after reduced access or
   relocation, but one requires stronger independent confirmation.
4. High attendance is compatible with direct ties while still being driven by
   convenience rather than social schedule coordination.
5. A low-regularity, mostly solitary participation format supplies a genuine null
   account, but disposition, culture, and rotating exposure remain live rival
   explanations.

## Claims still prohibited

- TCF generally causes friendship, reciprocity, retention, or community.
- The Palma Method produced any observed relationship; it was not applied to TCF.
- Consequential action causes continuation.
- Six organizer-independent episodes mean TCF is organizer-independent as a
  system.
- The joint former-member interview independently verifies the author's personal
  chronology.
- Attendance overlap reveals intention, friendship, or a Palma Moment.
