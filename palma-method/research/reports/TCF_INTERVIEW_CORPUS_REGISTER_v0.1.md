# TCF interview corpus register v0.1

## Start here

This is the canonical GitHub entry point for the TCF interview corpus as of
19 August 2026. Other agents should begin here, then open the referenced source
card. Full transcripts and audio stay outside GitHub by design.

**Availability is not publication permission.** Every interview remains subject
to its own consent, naming, quotation, third-party, and fact-check restrictions.
Real participant names, private attendance data, and sensitive stories must not be
reconstructed from context or added to manuscript prose without explicit clearance.

  member sources, and one joint former-member interview with two respondents.
- Seven member perspectives plus the founder, counting the joint interview as two

## How another agent should use this corpus

1. Start with this register and the SC card, not a chat summary.
2. Treat the private primary source as the evidentiary original and the SC card as
   a bounded derivative.
3. Keep four layers separate: participant statement, interviewer observation,
   project interpretation, and untested hypothesis.
4. Preserve negative cases and rival explanations.
5. Never infer causality from attendance overlap, self-description, or one vivid
   story.
6. Before manuscript use, check the card's consent and verification section.
7. Never reveal individual Wodify records or compare a respondent with attendance
   data in the interview itself.

## Corpus-level limits

- Recruitment is purposive and partly relationship-based, not representative.
- Interview formats and lengths differ.
- Consent and transcript quality are uneven.
- Several respondents know the interviewer personally.
- Founder accounts and participant accounts are not interchangeable.
- Private attendance data can support triangulation but cannot explain motives.
- The corpus can develop and challenge hypotheses; it does not establish the
  effectiveness of the Palma Method, which has not been applied to TCF.
