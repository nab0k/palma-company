# Author review change map — addendum v0.2

## How to read this addendum

This document extends, and does not replace,
`AUTHOR_REVIEW_CHANGE_MAP_v0.1.md`. The cumulative editorial instruction for the
next manuscript is v0.1 plus this addendum. If wording differs, the later founder
decision recorded here governs the next edit.

The Notion review page contained 45 unresolved discussions when reconciled:
42 founder comments and three assistant notes. All are accounted for below.

the proposed Alina/Timur expansion, and the proposed removal of the duplicate
