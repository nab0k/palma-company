8. revise all fifteen chapters before asking the founder to resume a complete
   line read.

## Recurring diagnoses

1. Scholars enter before the reader understands the question or has a reason to
   remember the name.
2. Positive claims are delayed behind manufactured negation.
3. Abstract explanations continue for several paragraphs without a person,
   object, episode, decision, or consequence.
4. Generic “we,” “the manager,” “the consultant,” and “usually” manufacture
   authority the author does not claim.
5. Research limitations repeat as defensive disclaimers instead of appearing
   once at the actual boundary.
6. Chapters 13 and 14 contain correct ingredients but lack a readable causal and
   narrative sequence.
7. New TCF interviews provide concrete counterexamples and variation that the
   manuscript has not yet used.
8. Classic theory needs contemporary continuation where newer work changes the
   mechanism, population, medium, or inference.

## Routing labels

- `rewrite_scholar_entry`: build question → person/work → material → finding →
  use → limit.
- `positive_claim_rewrite`: state Y first; restore X only as a live alternative.
- `clarity_rewrite`: rebuild the local logic around a concrete referent.
- `delete_or_compress`: the passage has not earned its place.
- `founder_edit_to_port`: compare the author's Notion edit and carry its meaning
  into GitHub without treating Notion as canonical.
- `interview_integration`: use only the merged, permission-aware TCF evidence.
- `research_gate`: verify before manuscript use.
- `local_edit`: bounded wording, punctuation, placement, or terminology repair.
