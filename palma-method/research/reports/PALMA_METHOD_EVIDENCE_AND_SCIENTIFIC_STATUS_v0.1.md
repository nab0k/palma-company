# Palma Method — Evidence and Scientific Status v0.1

Task: TASK_CLAUDE_0027, Stream 4
Owner: Claude
Status: draft for editorial review (Codex / Serhii)

This note separates, in compact form, what is established, what Palma
synthesizes, what Palma newly proposes, and what remains untested. It
consolidates material already developed across TASK_CLAUDE_0024-0026 and
now registered in `DECISION_0006` and `PALMA_METHOD_CANON_v1.1.md`, restated
here specifically for the conclusion's shortened scientific-status
paragraphs to point to (per `PART_IV_CONTENT_TRANSFER_MAP_v0.1.md`).

**Position, stated once and held throughout: defensible conceptual novelty
with untested propositions.** This note does not raise that position to
"preliminary empirical contribution" or to any claim of scientific
validation, and gives the specific reasons why not in each section below.

---

## 1. Established scientific foundations

The Method's component mechanisms are established, independently-sourced
research, each already carrying its own verified or checked-not-verified
source card in this project: Feld's social foci (opportunity allocation for
repeated contact); Collins's interaction ritual chains (emotional energy
from successful, repeated rituals); Granovetter, Burt, Coleman, and Portes
on tie strength, brokerage, closure, and closure's costs; Schein's process
consultation and Humble Inquiry; Selznick's institutionalization; Ostrom's
institutional-durability design principles (subject to the still-open VI-001
primary-verification restriction); Croskerry's premature closure; Brehm's
psychological reactance; Weick's sensemaking-collapse framework; and the
Dynes & Quarantelli emergent-organization typology. **None of these
findings originates with Palma Method, and none becomes Palma's own
discovery by being collected into one book.**

## 2. Palma's synthesis of existing theories

The five Relationship Capital dimensions, the four-stage cycle, and the
field-tool set connect research from separate traditions into one practical
sequence. This connection can be useful and internally consistent without
being, on its own, scientific confirmation of anything - a synthesis is an
editorial and practical achievement, not an empirical one. The Palma
Relationship Map's dyadic/network split legitimately draws on multi-layer
network-mapping practice (Krackhardt & Hanson); the Infrastructure Canvas's
five fields draw on Feld, Wenger, and Ostrom without claiming their
combination has itself been tested as a unit.

## 3. Palma's possible conceptual novelty

The narrow candidate contribution, stated precisely: **a link between a
consequential, participant-initiated action at the dyadic level and
distributed relational infrastructure at the system level, operationalized
specifically by whether continuation still requires the organizer to
recreate it.** This is narrower than "relationships matter" or "founders
create dependency" - both established, uncontested ideas - and narrower
even than "communities mature," since community-of-practice lifecycle
models (Wenger, McDermott & Snyder 2002) already describe organizational
maturation without making organizer-independence the operational pivot the
way this candidate does. See `PALMA_TRANSITION_MODEL_STRESS_TEST_v0.1.md`
and `PALMA_TRANSITION_MODEL_PRIOR_ART_CLOSURE_v0.1.md` for the full
falsification attempt this claim survived.

## 4. Untested propositions

Seven propositions (P1-P7, full detail in
`PALMA_METHOD_FALSIFIABLE_PROPOSITIONS_v0.1.md` and
`PALMA_TRANSITION_MODEL_STRESS_TEST_v0.1.md`) convert the strongest claims
into falsifiable form. None has been tested. The two sharpest - **P6**
(route-forming quality predicts durability better than raw action
frequency) and **P7** (distributed infrastructure mediates the link between
reciprocal action and readiness to respond) - remain **wholly untested**,
including exploratorily: the interview material this project holds does not
contain the episode-by-episode route detail either proposition would
require even for a first pass. Separately unresolved: whether the five
Relationship Capital dimensions are empirically discriminant (P4); whether
Response Signals form a real sequence or behave independently (P3); and
whether a Handoff Test's result generalizes beyond its observed window and
function (implicit throughout §11 of the field-guide appendix).

## 5. What the TCF exploratory coding showed and could not show

Five existing interviews (SC-095 through SC-099), one coder, no second
rater, no comparison group, a deliberately selected contrast case, and an
interviewer who also authored the model. **What it showed**: the model's
basic elements (an unscripted, consequential, peer-directed action) were
present in four of five accounts and explicitly, directly absent in the
fifth - the designed contrast case - which is consistent with the model's
basic logic. **What it could not show**: any statistical pattern (five
cases support counts, not correlations); anything about "TCF members" as a
class (non-random, non-representative selection); or either P6 or P7,
which the coding pass did not and could not address. **What it additionally
exposed**: a genuine construct problem - "continued peer relationship
outside scheduled training" could not be cleanly separated from ordinary
gym-adjacent contact in two of the five interviews, a finding about
measurement precision, not about the model's truth or falsity. Full record:
`TCF_TRANSITION_MODEL_EXPLORATORY_CODING_v0.1.md`.

## 6. Limitations of the prior-art search

The search across network-formation, network-governance, relational-event,
and founder-succession literatures (`PALMA_TRANSITION_MODEL_PRIOR_ART_
CLOSURE_v0.1.md`) was **targeted and bounded, not systematic**. Of six newly
checked primary sources, **full text could not be retrieved or read for
four** - two blocked by access restrictions, two by PDF-parsing failures in
the tooling available at the time. Only one source (Provan & Kenis, 2008)
reached publisher-abstract-level verification, the strongest tier this
search achieved. **"No equivalent integrated model was found" is a bounded
negative-search result, not a claim that no such model exists anywhere in
the world's literature.** Community-organizing and stakeholder-engagement
prior-art gaps, flagged as early as `PALMA_METHOD_PRIOR_ART_MATRIX_v0.1.md`,
remain open and uninvestigated.

## 7. Minimal future validation program

Not a description of work completed - a proposed sequence, cheapest steps
first, per `PALMA_METHOD_FALSIFIABLE_PROPOSITIONS_v0.1.md`'s existing
validation path:

1. Independent second-coder pass over the existing five TCF interviews,
   computing real inter-rater agreement for the Palma Moment and
   route-of-continuation constructs specifically - the cheapest step
   available, requiring no new data collection.
2. A small, prospective pilot at one bounded site, tracking
   participant-initiated actions and resulting routes forward in time rather
   than reconstructing them retrospectively.
3. Primary-text verification of the four network-methods papers this task's
   search could not read in full, closing the specific gap named in §6.
4. Multi-item scale development for the five Relationship Capital
   dimensions, tested for basic discriminant validity (P4) before any
   measurement claim is made.
5. A genuine Handoff Test, run prospectively with a preselected function,
   natural-rhythm window, and bounded founder availability - something no
   case in this project's evidence base has yet had.

**No part of this program should be described, in the manuscript or
elsewhere, as already completed.** The position this note holds -
defensible conceptual novelty with untested propositions - is exactly the
distance between where the Method currently stands and where this program
would need to take it.
