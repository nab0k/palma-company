## 2. Alcoholics Anonymous

- **Proposed chapter:** 1 (Vipassana).
- **Exact existing passage this could pair with:** the paragraph ending
  "...Сильное переживание и работающая инфраструктура — разные вещи.
  Первое может вызвать желание помочь; вторая превращает это желание в
  понятную роль, повторяемый порядок и следующий курс," immediately
  before "Випассана дала мне сразу два наблюдения." AA belongs as a short
  aside inserted at or after this point - the chapter has just finished
  explaining Vipassana's *own* route to founder-independent continuation
  (standardized, recorded teaching plus an assistant-teacher hierarchy);
  AA is the ready-made structural contrast (radical decentralization
  instead).
- **What should be removed if inserted:** nothing - a genuine addition,
  not a replacement. Already recommended by TASK_CLAUDE_0031 at this exact
  location and not yet inserted.
- **Personal story / theoretical block to pair with:** the chapter's own
  closing observation that Vipassana "может десятилетиями воспроизводиться
  далеко от основателя, когда участникам доступны роли, правила, места и
  сохранённая память" - AA pairs directly as the opposite-mechanism case
  for the same outcome.
- **Requires a new source card:** no - `alcoholics-anonymous-decentralized-
  governance.md` (SC-137) already exists from TASK_CLAUDE_0031.
- **Estimated word effect:** +150 to +250 (short contrast).
- **Duplication risk:** none - the only candidate solving Chapter 1's
  founder-independence question by the opposite mechanism.

## 4. Occupy Sandy

- **Proposed chapter:** 11 (TAK.Shtab).
- **Exact existing passage this could pair with:** the paragraph
  discussing Dynes & Quarantelli's four-type typology - "Если и люди, и
  порядок работы собираются только из-за катастрофы, возникает новая,
  эмерджентная группа" - and the following paragraph's own caution that
  "Типология появилась из полевых исследований природных бедствий, а не
  войны, и не предсказывает успех... реальные случаи смешивают
  категории." Occupy Sandy belongs as a one- or two-sentence real-world
  named example immediately after this passage - a second, independently
  documented "emergent" organization whose subsequent institutional
  trajectory diverged sharply from TAK.Shtab's, which sharpens rather than
  dilutes the chapter's own point that the typology "не предсказывает
  успех."
- **What should be removed if inserted:** nothing - a brief addition to an
  existing theoretical passage, not a replacement of any TAK.Shtab material
  (DECISION_0007 explicitly requires TAK.Shtab be told once, without
  competing case weight, and this map respects that).
- **Personal story / theoretical block to pair with:** the Dynes &
  Quarantelli passage specifically, already present.
- **Requires a new source card:** already created this task -
  `occupy-sandy-2012-emergent-mutual-aid.md` (SC-148).
- **Estimated word effect:** +100 to +250 (short contrast only - a longer
  treatment risks exactly the duplication this task's own brief warned
  against).
- **Duplication risk:** moderate if told at length; low if kept to one or
  two sentences as an explicit contrast, per this task's own instruction.

## 7. Mondragon

- **Proposed chapter:** 13 (Дизайн) or 15 (Передача) - genuinely the
  weakest-fitting placement of the seven cases this map recommends
  considering.
- **Exact existing passage this could pair with:** Chapter 13 and Chapter
  15 were both read in full for this map. Neither currently has an obvious
  gap shaped for an international case - Chapter 13 builds its Design-
  stage argument through the Infrastructure Canvas and a hypothetical
  founder/manager example; Chapter 15 builds its Transfer-stage argument
  through TAK.Shtab, Stinchcombe's organizational-imprinting research, and
  Pearce & Conger's distributed-leadership research, closing directly into
  the book's own scientific-status statement. Inserting Mondragon into
  either chapter would require either displacing existing theoretical
  material or adding genuine new length, not a clean substitution.
- **What should be removed if inserted:** most likely candidate, if the
  Chief Editor wants Mondragon in Chapter 15, is trimming part of the
  Stinchcombe/imprinting discussion - but this map does not recommend that
  trim; it flags the absence of a clean slot as the honest finding.
- **Personal story / theoretical block to pair with:** institutionalization-
  beyond-founder-death, thematically close to but distinct from TAK.Shtab's
  own institutionalization material (Selznick) already present in Chapter
  11.
- **Requires a new source card:** already created this task -
  `mondragon-cooperative-federation-governance.md` (SC-150).
- **Estimated word effect:** +500 to +800 if given real room; the case
  cannot be honestly told in under ~300 words given the international-
  subsidiary complication this task's own audit flagged.
- **Duplication risk:** low functionally, but real risk of *editorial*
  redundancy with TAK.Shtab's own institutionalization material if not
  clearly differentiated (federation-scale, multi-decade, post-founder-
  death vs. TAK.Shtab's single-organization, few-years, founder-still-
  present case).
