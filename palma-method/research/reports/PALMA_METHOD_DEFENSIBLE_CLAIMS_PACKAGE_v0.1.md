# Palma Method — Defensible Claims Package v0.1

Task: TASK_CLAUDE_0024, Stream 6
Owner: Claude
Status: draft for editorial review (Codex / Serhii)

This package recommends exact language for the eight items the task brief
requests, then addresses the candidate position statement and the Relationship
Capital naming question directly. All recommended language below is
deliberately conservative relative to what the other four deliverables in this
task found - anywhere this package is more cautious than the underlying audit,
that is intentional: claims language should trail the evidence, not lead it.

---

## 1. What Palma Method is

**Recommended language:** "Palma Method is a practitioner framework for
diagnosing whether an organization's reach has become genuine readiness to
respond, designing conditions for participants to create value with each other
rather than only with the organizer, and testing what continues after the
organizer steps back. It draws on established research in social capital,
network theory, interaction ritual theory, and organizational consulting, and
organizes that research into a practical sequence a practitioner can follow."

**Not recommended:** "a scientifically validated system," "a proven method," or
any language implying the sequence itself, as opposed to its component
research, has been independently tested.

---

## 2. What Relationship Capital means in this book

**Recommended language:** "Relationship Capital is the value accumulated in an
organization's relationships that enables trust, cooperation, access, support,
and coordinated action - assessed across five dimensions (trust, reciprocity,
access, relationship strength, and network structure) at either the level of a
single relationship or the organization's whole portfolio of relationships.
This is this book's own working synthesis, not a claim to represent the single
settled definition in the academic literature."

**Not recommended:** presenting the five dimensions as an exhaustive,
field-consensus taxonomy, or omitting the "this book's own synthesis" framing
DECISION_0003 itself requires.

---

## 3. How the concept relates to social and relational capital research

**Recommended language:** "Relationship Capital draws directly on decades of
social-capital research (Bourdieu, Coleman, Putnam, Portes) and on the
narrower academic term 'relational capital' used in interorganizational
alliance research (Kale, Singh & Perlmutter). It is not identical to either:
it is scoped to identifiable relationships an organization or individual
actually participates in, assessed through five specific dimensions this book
selects and combines for practitioner use."

**Not recommended:** claiming Palma "resolves" or "supersedes" any academic
debate in this literature, or implying the five-dimension model has itself been
subjected to the same empirical scrutiny as its component sources.

---

## 4. What is original about Palma Method

**Recommended language:** "Palma's original contribution is the combination and
operationalization: naming access and relationship strength as first-class,
equally-weighted dimensions alongside trust, reciprocity, and network structure;
the Palma Moment as a specific, nameable event to watch for in real time; and a
practitioner sequence (diagnosis, design, launch, and testing what transfers)
built to apply these ideas to a specific organization's situation."

**Not recommended:** claiming originality for any individual mechanism
(reciprocity, weak ties, closure, interaction ritual, institutionalization) that
this package's own prior-art matrix traces to a named academic predecessor.

---

## 5. What is not claimed as original

**Recommended language, as an explicit list for any front-matter or methods
appendix:** "This book does not claim to have discovered that relationships
create value, that dense networks both help and harm their members, that weak
ties carry novel information, that shared foci organize who becomes close to
whom, that successful rituals build emotional energy, or that organizational
value can transfer away from the individuals who built it. Each of these is
established research this book cites by name and applies."

**Not recommended:** silence on this point - per the candidate position
statement assessed in Section 9 below, stating plainly what is *not* claimed is
itself part of a defensible position, not a weakness to minimize.

---

## 6. What has and has not been scientifically validated

**Recommended language:** "No part of Palma Method - including the five
dimensions, the Palma Moment, the Response Signals, the Relationship Capital
Map, the Infrastructure Canvas, or the Handoff Test - has been independently,
empirically validated as a measurement instrument or a tested causal model. The
component research it draws on (cited throughout) has, in most cases, been
independently validated in its own original context; Palma's application of
that research to a specific practitioner framework has not."

**Not recommended:** "evidence-based," "proven effective," "scientifically
validated," or "a validated psychometric scale" applied to any of the five
tools or the five-dimension model, under any circumstance, per this task's own
forbidden list.

---

## 7. How TCF, TAK.Shtab, and Bombom should be described

**Recommended language, one line per case, for consistent use throughout any
future manuscript, marketing, or methods material:**

- **TCF:** "a developing field case, illustrated through individual member
  interviews, each cited to its own source - not a representative sample of
  TCF's membership, and not a controlled study."
- **TAK.Shtab:** "the project's most independently corroborated case, since its
  institutionalization is documented on the organization's own public page,
  independent of the founder's personal account - still a single case, not a
  controlled test, and not described as having undergone any formally designed
  test."
- **Bombom:** "a single, still-unfolding case used to illustrate the difference
  between an audience and a community in real time - explicitly not concluded
  to be a community, per the manuscript's own honest finding."

**Classification against the task's own five-way scheme (illustration /
exploratory case / hypothesis-generating case / test / evidence):** all three
are best described as **illustration and hypothesis-generating case material**.
None should be described as a **test** of any proposition in
`PALMA_METHOD_FALSIFIABLE_PROPOSITIONS_v0.1.md`, and none should be described as
independent **evidence** for any Palma-specific claim (as opposed to evidence
for the underlying, independently-established academic sources each case
illustrates).

---

## 8. What may be said on the book cover, Amazon page, and website

**Recommended language:** "The Palma Method: a practical framework, grounded in
social-capital and network research, for turning reach into real relationships
- diagnosing what's actually happening, designing conditions for people to
connect with each other, and building infrastructure that doesn't depend on one
person forever."

**Not recommended anywhere in cover/marketing copy:** "scientifically proven,"
"validated method," "guaranteed," any specific unqualified statistic drawn from
a single case (e.g., TAK.Shtab's follower count or funding figures) presented as
evidence the Method works, or "the science of relationship capital" phrased as
though Palma's specific model is itself an established scientific finding rather
than a synthesis built on established findings.

## 9. The candidate position statement — accept, revise, or reject

**The statement as given:** "Palma Method does not claim to discover that
relationships create value. Its contribution is to organize established
insights into a practical system for diagnosing when reach has - or has not -
become readiness to respond, designing conditions for participant-to-participant
action, observing the signals of that change, and transferring part of the
system beyond its initiator."

**Assessment: accept, with one addition.** This position is well-calibrated
against everything this audit found. It correctly (a) disclaims discovery of
the underlying mechanism, (b) correctly names the four real activities the
Method performs (diagnosing, designing, observing, transferring), and (c)
avoids every overclaim this audit flagged elsewhere (validation, discriminant
proof, a single settled architecture). It is, if anything, slightly more
conservative than some of the manuscript's own current framing (e.g., Chapter
21's TAK.Shtab sentence, flagged in the sync map), which is the right direction
for a position statement to err.

**The one addition recommended:** the statement should note, explicitly, that
the practical system it describes currently exists in more than one internally
consistent form (see the phase-architecture divergence, Section 1.1 of the sync
map) and that this is a known, open item pending resolution - not because the
position statement needs to resolve it, but because a statement this precise
about what Palma "is" should not imply a settled architecture the project's own
governing documents don't yet agree on. Suggested addition, appended to the
statement: "The specific sequence through which this happens is still being
finalized editorially and should not yet be presented as fixed."

## 10. The Relationship Capital naming decision — evaluation, not a silent replacement

**The proposed direction, as given:** "The Palma Method" as the principal brand;
"relationship capital" as a descriptive central concept, not a claimed
proprietary term; Palma's novelty located in the method and operational
architecture, not in inventing the phrase.

**Evaluation, treating the four sub-questions separately as the brief requests:**

- **Academic/conceptual use inside the book:** this direction is fully
  supported by this audit. The manuscript already uses "Relationship Capital"
  descriptively, cites the narrower academic "relational capital" tradition
  correctly once the SC-117/SC-118 cluster is drawn on (per
  `THEORY_EXPANSION_PLACEMENT_MAP_v0.2.md` item 2c), and never claims to have
  coined the underlying scientific idea. **Accept as-is.**
- **Use in the book title or subtitle:** "The Palma Method" as the primary
  title, with "relationship capital" available for subtitle or descriptive use,
  is consistent with the corrected trademark finding (SC-118, corrected in this
  task): the 2007-2008 Nour application never registered and was abandoned, so
  it does not itself bar this usage - but a still-unresolved, newer 2022
  application for the same phrase by a different applicant (Serial No.
  97601136, flagged but not investigated in this task's Nour correction) means
  "clear to use" is not yet established either. **Accept the direction; do not
  treat this audit's correction as clearance to proceed without the legal
  review already recommended in SC-118.**
- **Use as the name of a commercial method, test, course, or consultancy
  service:** higher risk than book-title use, since a registered mark for
  consulting services specifically (which is exactly what the abandoned 2007
  Nour application, and possibly the new 2022 application, cover) would be more
  directly relevant to a commercial "Relationship Capital" course or service
  than to a book title. **This specific sub-question requires the professional
  trademark-clearance review this project has recommended and not yet
  performed - this audit does not and cannot resolve it.**
- **Trademark registration and freedom-to-operate:** explicitly out of scope for
  this or any prior research task in this project. **No claim is made here
  about clearance.**

**Explicit statement per the task's own instruction:** an abandoned trademark
application is not treated in this audit as a prohibition on using the phrase,
and it is equally not treated as legal clearance to use it. Both framings would
overreach what a desk-research correction can establish.

**Overall evaluation of the proposed direction: reasonable and consistent with
this audit's findings, not silently replaced.** The one qualification worth
carrying forward explicitly: the newer 2022 trademark application surfaced
during this task's correction work is unresolved and should be checked before
any commercial (as opposed to book-title or academic) use of the phrase moves
forward.
