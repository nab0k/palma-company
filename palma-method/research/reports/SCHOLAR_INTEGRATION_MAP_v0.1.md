# Scholar integration map — manuscript v0.10

## Purpose

This map records the first substantive entrance of each scholar or research
group expanded in manuscript v0.10. It is an editorial trace, not a substitute
for source cards. Each entrance is designed to answer five reader questions:
who these people are, what problem led to the work, what they actually studied
or built, what the work changes in the chapter, and where its authority ends.

## Relocation and repetition decisions

- Granovetter and Burt no longer receive abbreviated biographies in Chapter 7
  before their full Chapter 10 entrance. The earlier chapter retains the ordinary
  distinction between closeness and a bridge without naming the scholars.
- Later uses of Weiss, Krackhardt and Hanson, Lave and Wenger, Schein, Coleman,
  Bourdieu, Granovetter, and Burt act as applications or callbacks and do not
  repeat their biographies.
- Incidental coauthors are named only where authorship of the central study is
  materially relevant. Names that merely created a second unexplained entrance
  were removed from the prose.

## Editorial status

All passages are integrated into the continuous manuscript rather than stored
as detachable mini-essays. They remain revision text for founder line review;
acceptance of this report does not accept every sentence of the manuscript.
