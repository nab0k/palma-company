# v0.8 claim-to-source integration map v0.1

## Rule

This map controls factual integration into v0.8. A card's stored status is
preserved verbatim. `checked` is enough to guide cautious working prose but not to
support an exact quotation, disputed legal conclusion, universal causal statement,
or prestige claim. Exact numbers and memorable examples are omitted or qualified
until the card itself supplies a traceable passage from an adequate source.
