## Supplemental author observation

SC-046 records Serhii's observation that Miguel often asks members for merchandise
opinions. It can corroborate the existence of a practice, but it does not measure
frequency or establish member governance. It must remain separate from the sensitive
post-loss merchandise lead.

## Use protocol

1. Start with this register and the source card, then consult the private primary
   source only when authorized.
2. Keep participant statement, author observation, interpretation, and hypothesis
   separate.
3. Do not infer motives or friendship from attendance overlap.
4. Check consent, names, quotations, third parties, and facts before manuscript use.
5. The corpus develops and challenges hypotheses; it does not prove TCF or the Palma
   Method effective.
