# Palma plant → callback → payoff ledger v0.1

## Purpose

This is the book's cross-chapter memory. It records details, questions, images,
scenes and theoretical promises to which the manuscript has given unusual weight.
It does not require every cup, chair or street to return. A detail enters this
ledger when its specificity, repetition, placement or emphasis makes a later
reader expect it to matter.

The ledger is an editorial control, not manuscript prose. No proposed destination
below is approved until the founder accepts it in context.

## Vocabulary

- `PLANT`: a detail or question whose presentation creates an expectation.
- `CALLBACK`: a recognisable return that restores the earlier material to memory.
- `PAYOFF`: a return that changes the reader's understanding, rather than merely
  repeating the detail.
- `MOTIF`: an image or action that can carry different meanings across returns.
- `RESERVE`: useful material with no approved prose destination yet.
- `SEMANTIC DEBT`: a claim, question or distinction that the book promises to
  convert into understanding and has not yet completed.
- `ATMOSPHERE`: a detail whose local work is sufficient; it needs no return.
- `DUPLICATE PAYOFF`: a later repetition that claims a new conclusion without
  adding one.

Proposed actions are `KEEP`, `RETURN`, `MOVE`, `DEVELOP`, `CUT`, `VERIFY`, and
`LEAVE AS ATMOSPHERE`.

## Mandatory chapter consultation gate

Once this ledger is accepted, every chapter task from the midpoint onward must
begin with a continuity check before research or prose work:

1. Name the exact chapter control and candidate sources.
2. List every `INBOUND` ledger item that the chapter may repay.
3. List every new `OUTBOUND` plant the chapter would create.
4. State which motif already performs the intended job before introducing another.
5. For each proposed callback, explain the new meaning it adds. Recognition alone
   is not a payoff.
6. Confirm that a current-control plant actually exists; reservoir text cannot be
   treated as already read by the audience.
7. At chapter handoff, update the item state: `PLANTED`, `RETURNED`, `PAID`,
   `DEFERRED`, `CUT`, or `ATMOSPHERE`.
8. Do not open a second centre in the chapter merely to service an old detail.

Required prompt line for all later chapter chats:

> Before changing this chapter, consult the accepted Palma Plant–Callback–Payoff
> Ledger. Report the chapter's inbound payoffs, outbound plants, motif collisions
> and semantic debts. Do not invent a callback or assume that reservoir prose is
> already present in the reader's control text.
