Format: self-administered, written (no live interviewer)
Estimated time to complete: 25–35 minutes
Status: draft
Related source: SC-045 (Café Bombom founder interview, 3 August 2026)

## Why this interview exists

SC-045 already establishes the frame: Bombom shows owner-to-customer
relational capital and offers of help, but not yet evidence of a
customer-to-customer community. The first interview left seven explicit
verification gaps open (SC-045, "Priority verification"). This continuation
turns those seven gaps into direct written questions, plus the question that
tests the Chapter 2 thesis on Sandra herself rather than inferring it from
the first account.

## Note on written format

This is a written follow-up, not a recorded conversation — there is no
interviewer present to probe in real time. Each question below has been
written to stand on its own, with the usual live probes folded in as prompts
so Sandra can address them directly in her answer. If a reply is too brief
to use on a given point, a short written follow-up may be sent afterward.

## Questions

1. **Timeline.** Can you walk me through the timeline — from the moment you
   realized something was wrong, to the day you closed, to the day you
   reopened? Roughly how much time passed at each stage (first sign of
   trouble → decision to close → reopening)?

2. **Offers versus realized help.** You mentioned customers offered
   contacts, professional help, maybe money. Which of those offers actually
   turned into something concrete — a call that led somewhere, an
   introduction that worked? Please give at least one specific example that
   led somewhere, and one that stayed just words, if you can recall one of
   each.

3. **Investors.** The four or five people who ended up investing — how did
   you know them before this happened? Were they already close friends or
   family, or more distant acquaintances who stepped up unexpectedly? Did
   they offer, or did you ask? What role do they play now — do they have a
   say in how the café runs day to day?

4. **Audience versus community (central question).** Did any of your
   regular customers know each other before Bombom — or did they meet at
   Bombom and go on to have their own relationship, independent of you? Was
   there ever a specific case where two regulars did something together, or
   arranged something with each other, without you being the connector? If
   yes, please describe what happened and how you found out about it. If
   you're not sure, is that something you think you'd know about if it had
   happened?

5. **Rhythm.** Is there anything at Bombom that repeats every week or every
   day the same way — a ritual that existed before the crisis, or one that
   emerged out of it?

6. **Governance after reopening.** What actually changed in how decisions
   get made day to day, compared with before the crisis?

7. Is there anything I haven't asked that you think matters?
