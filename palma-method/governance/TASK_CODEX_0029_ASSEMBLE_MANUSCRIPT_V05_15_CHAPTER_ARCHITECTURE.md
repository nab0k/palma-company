# TASK_CODEX_0029 — Assemble manuscript v0.5 in fifteen chapters

## Objective

Create an append-only Russian successor to manuscript v0.4 using the architecture
approved in DECISION_0007. Consolidate the lived cases, remove rejected material,
reallocate theory, and preserve the distinction between established findings,
observations, hypotheses, and proposed Palma procedures.

## Phases

### Phase 1 — structural assembly

- Create the fifteen-chapter reading order without overwriting v0.4.
- Remove Bombom and the other rejected cases from the reading manuscript.
- Consolidate TCF, TAK.Shtab, Kichkine, Anton/publishing, and Relationship Capital
  material into their approved canonical homes.
- Remove duplicate introductions and chapter-number navigation.
- Preserve unresolved evidence or privacy boundaries as editorial markers outside
  the reader-facing prose.

### Phase 2 — theory and case reconstruction

- Rebuild scholar introductions around a practical question and actual finding.
- Rehome Wenger and Coleman/Portes after Bombom's removal.
- Select or commission only the external cases required by a specific chapter
  mechanism.
- Incorporate independently checked Vipassana evidence only after source-card
  traceability is established.

### Phase 3 — Method chapters and integrity pass

- Keep Diagnose, Design, Launch, and Transfer consistent with DECISION_0006.
- Do not freeze the Canvas field count before a founder decision on the two audited
  options.
- Remove claims that Palma was applied to TCF or formally tested through TAK.Shtab.
- Complete evidence, privacy, public-safety, terminology, repetition, and voice
  review.

## Allowed changes

- Add the append-only manuscript v0.5 and its transfer/evidence log.
- Reuse, move, shorten, and minimally revise v0.4 prose under DECISION_0007.
- Add sourced passages required to complete the four major lived cases.
- Add task, decision, and completion-report records listed in `output_paths`.

## Forbidden changes

- Do not overwrite or delete manuscript v0.4, accepted chapter versions, source
  cards, previous decisions, or research reports.
- Do not change the canonical four-stage Method architecture.
- Do not describe Palma Method or its tools as validated, proven, or already
  applied to TCF.
- Do not infer third-party motives, identify private participants without
  permission, or expose operationally sensitive wartime details.
- Do not silently choose a four- or five-field Infrastructure Canvas.
- Do not synchronize to Notion or Google Docs and do not merge without founder
  approval.

## Acceptance criteria

1. One complete Russian manuscript successor contains fifteen numbered chapters,
   a conclusion, and clearly separated appendices.
2. Bombom and the explicitly rejected corporate mini-cases are absent from the
   reader-facing manuscript.
3. TCF and TAK.Shtab each have one complete canonical account; later uses add a new
   analytical layer without reintroduction.
4. Each chapter supplies a legible reader gain: distinction, mechanism, bounded
   proposition, procedure, or consequence.
5. Each retained scholar and international case has a specific argumentative job.
6. Unsupported causal, scientific-status, validation, and future-research claims
   are removed or corrected.
7. All changed factual passages are traceable in the transfer/evidence log.
8. The exact diff and all unresolved publication blockers are presented before any
   synchronization or merge.

discovery dossier on Goenka Vipassana. It is not itself treated as evidence. Its
primary and academic references must be checked and registered before factual
