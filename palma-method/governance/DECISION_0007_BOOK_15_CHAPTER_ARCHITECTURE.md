# DECISION_0007 — Fifteen-chapter book architecture

## Founder-directed decision

The next Russian manuscript successor will be rebuilt around fifteen numbered
chapters. The book is an intellectual and research journey for founders and
leaders, not a sales narrative controlled by the Palma Method product.

The governing movement is:

> communication → consequential response → relationship → network → community →
> relational infrastructure → transfer

The manuscript should give a founder or leader a way to examine relational
resources, distinguish an audience from a community, and begin designing,
observing, and testing conditions under which people act with and for one another.

## Working chapter architecture

1. **Vipassana** — a personal opening and the transition from participant to
   volunteer role and reproducible infrastructure.
2. **The Sound of One Hand Clapping** — response as the relational unit of
   communication and the origin of the Palma Moment hypothesis.
3. **Borrowed Access** — reputation, legitimacy, trust, and access are not the
   same resource.
4. **What Accumulates in Relationships** — Relationship Capital, renewal,
   mobilizability, and institutionalization.
5. **The Collective Fitness** — one full chronological account from the author's
   entry into the gym through relationships and consequences outside it.
6. **Repeated Encounters** — social foci, interaction ritual chains, and the
   limits of what attendance data can show.
7. **Whom Can You Trust with a Child?** — Kichkine and the problem of defining
   community.
8. **From First Users to Community** — where community belongs in the life of a
   product or organization and when it is not required.
9. **Whom the Community Did Not Notice** — boundaries, exclusion, peripheral
   participants, and the dark side of cohesion.
10. **If One Point Disappears** — Anton Nosik, the publishing-house failure, hubs,
    brokers, and network vulnerability.
11. **TAK.Shtab** — one complete chronological case from pre-existing relations
    through mobilization, role formation, institutionalization, and the founder's
    changed role.
12. **Diagnose** — define the needed action and the correct unit of analysis.
13. **Design** — use a bounded, theory-informed Infrastructure Canvas.
14. **Launch** — run a bounded intervention and observe Response Signals without
    confusing correlation with causation.
15. **Transfer** — use a bounded Handoff Test and assemble the Palma Method as the
    provisional result of the book's inquiry.

Chapter titles remain editorially adjustable without changing this decision's
functional architecture. The conclusion and evidence/practice appendices sit
outside the fifteen numbered chapters.

## Narrative allocation

- TCF is a longitudinal research laboratory. Chapter 5 tells the full baseline;
  later appearances add a new question or evidence layer and do not reintroduce
  the case.
- TAK.Shtab is told once, chronologically, in Chapter 11. Later Method chapters may
  use a short self-contained episode but may not retell the case or rely on a bare
  chapter-number reference.
- Kichkine is a lived case of overlapping work, neighborhood, and family networks
  within the South Coast environment of the author's childhood. The author has
  approved the general story, the kindergarten escape, the Ai-Petri trip, and the
  everyday workplace scenes for working-draft use. The teacher's offer after the
  author's family loss remains subject to approval of the exact public wording.
- Anton Nosik remains a public named figure within Chapter 10 rather than receiving
  a standalone chapter. Any claim about how others characterized him after death
  requires a public source.
- Vipassana is not presented as automatic proof that a retreat creates community.
  It is a case of a standardized event connected to volunteer pathways, local
  organizations, durable assets, recorded knowledge, and delegated roles.

## Removals and case discipline

- Bombom is removed from the main manuscript. The organization did not require a
  customer community for the business outcome described, so using its absence as
  failure would be a category error. Research materials remain archived.
- Dewey & LeBoeuf, Nvidia/OpenAI, Chief, Abercrombie & Fitch, CrowdStrike/Delta, and
  Zoho are removed from the next build unless a later founder decision restores a
  precisely bounded use.
- The final book should prefer six to eight substantial international cases,
  ideally arranged as contrasts, over many interchangeable miniatures.
- An external case remains only when it demonstrates the exact relational
  mechanism, is memorable, is strongly sourced, and cannot be replaced more
  effectively by a lived or clearly hypothetical example.

## Theory and prose consequences

- The manuscript will build a sequential synthesis using established research as
  support. It will not claim that the Palma integration is validated science.
- A scholar's first appearance must explain the practical problem, the work
  performed, the finding, the bridge to the current argument, and any material
  limit. Theory must answer a managerial question rather than decorate the prose.
- Bare internal references such as “in Chapter X” are removed. When prior context
  is necessary, the relevant fact is restated in a self-contained sentence.
- Defensive qualification should mark the boundary of a positive claim rather
  than replace the claim.

## Deferred decisions

This decision does not settle:

1. whether the Infrastructure Canvas has four or five fields;
2. the final operational definition of community, including the editorial
   three-person threshold;
3. the final six-to-eight international cases;
4. final public wording for the sensitive Kichkine teacher scene;
5. English translation choices.

These items may remain open during structural assembly but must be resolved before
publication of the affected passages.
