# DECISION_0009 — v0.8 editorial scope and international-case portfolio

## Editorial objective

The next append-only Russian working manuscript will be v0.8. It will retain the
fifteen-chapter architecture and make the book more coherent, useful, learned, and
recognizably authored. It is a Russian editorial build toward an English-language
book; no Russian publication is planned.

The book is a substantial intellectual argument and a visible research journey for
founders and leaders who need an audience, supporters, testers, early adopters,
partners, investors, or communication that leads to action. The intended result is
that a reader can assess relational resources and begin designing, testing, and
analyzing relationships or community conditions.

The working narrative allocation remains approximately:

- theory and research: 40 percent;
- international cases: 25 percent;
- TCF: 20 percent;
- the author's other lived stories: 15 percent.

These are editorial proportions, not mechanical quotas.

## Method and evidence position

Palma Method is the name of the author's practical framework, assembled
retrospectively from publishing, marketing, advocacy, civil-society work,
TAK.Shtab, TCF, interviews, and ordinary observations. TCF made the proposed
mechanisms unusually visible; it did not originate or validate them. Earlier cases
must not be rewritten as if the Method caused their results.

Research provides language, comparison, and limits. The manuscript will not claim
that the Method, Palma Moment, Infrastructure Canvas, Response Signals, or Handoff
Test are scientifically validated. Appendix 2 will explain how the framework was
assembled instead of arguing defensively for novelty or scientific status.

The official tool name is **Palma Method Infrastructure Canvas**. After its first
formal introduction, the prose may use **the Infrastructure Canvas**. Its four
fields remain Paths, Roles, Rhythms, and Memory under DECISION_0008.

## International-case portfolio

The v0.8 build uses a small portfolio in which each case performs a distinct job.
Narrative ranges are planning guides, not publication requirements.

1. **Harley Owners Group (H.O.G.) — retain as a full case.** It shows deliberate
   participant infrastructure around a commercial product and remains the main
   brand-community case.
2. **Dewey & LeBoeuf — restore for a bounded use of roughly 600–900 words.** It is
   not a community case. Paired with the author's publishing-house story, it shows
   what happens when client relationships and institutional continuity remain
   attached to mobile professionals. Do not claim that the firm had no other client
   routes unless primary evidence supports that claim. This ruling supersedes only
   DECISION_0007's removal of Dewey & LeBoeuf.
3. **parkrun — add as a 400–600-word case.** Its job is to show a repeatable local
   event, volunteer roles, a portable operating model, and distributed continuity.
4. **Alcoholics Anonymous — add as a 150–250-word structural contrast after
   Vipassana.** Compare repeatability, local autonomy, service roles, and textual
   memory. Do not make treatment-effect or clinical claims, and do not describe AA
   as a pure opposite of Vipassana.
5. **Wikipedia — add as a 250–400-word boundary/governance case after the exact
   studies and governance facts are verified.** Stack Overflow remains a reserve;
   the two must not perform the same job in the same build. Wikimedia community and
   Foundation authority must not be flattened into one actor.
6. **Apple/Google/Intel/Adobe no-poach coordination — retain at medium length.** It
   is a dark-side case showing that elite relational coordination can create value
   for insiders while imposing costs on excluded people. Legal claims require
   primary-record verification and careful attribution.
7. **CrossFit — retain only as a 250–400-word structural comparison.** Use it to
   distinguish an affiliated global format from one local gym's relationships. Do
   not include the Glassman controversy in the current build. Do not call TCF a
   CrossFit affiliate or “CrossFit-style” without the author's confirmation.

**Remove from the current build:** Google+, Salesforce, Occupy Sandy, and Mondragon.
The Salesforce “Paul Adler” founder claim is prohibited as false. Chief,
Abercrombie & Fitch, CrowdStrike/Delta, Zoho, Nvidia/OpenAI, and Bombom remain
removed under DECISION_0007. Removed research remains traceable and may be reused
only after a new bounded decision.

## Theory and narrative rules

- A scholar's first appearance must explain the practical question, what was
  studied, what was found, why it matters here, and the material limitation.
- Later appearances should recall the idea briefly instead of repeating a biography.
- Memorable examples and numbers enter prose only after claim-level verification.
- Six to eight substantial international cases are preferred to interchangeable
  miniatures. Cases should be paired or contrasted where the comparison adds a
  precise mechanism.
- TCF is a longitudinal field laboratory. Its first appearance must give readers a
  physical and social scene before analytical use.
- TAK.Shtab receives one coherent chronological account. Later uses must be bounded
  and must not imply retrospective application of Palma Method.
- Bare “as discussed in Chapter X” references are removed. Needed context is restated.
- Reduce the repeated “not X but Y” construction, defensive caveats, generic
  management hypotheticals, and machine-smoothed prose. Restore concrete detail,
  authorial judgment, rhythm, and humor.

## Community position

Community remains a strong or ideal form in the book, with explicit qualifications.
Relationships and networks can exist before community, and useful relational
capital does not require every organization to build one. The manuscript should
name relevant categories rather than use “community” for every recurring group.
