# DECISION_0008 — Four-field Palma Infrastructure Canvas

## Founder-directed decision

The canonical working Palma Infrastructure Canvas has four fields:

1. **Paths** — how people find one another and where the next interaction can
   continue without the founder recreating the connection.
2. **Roles** — what a participant is authorized and equipped to do beyond
   receiving a service or instruction.
3. **Rhythms** — what repeats without a fresh appeal from the center.
4. **Memory** — what the system can retain, retrieve, and use without another
   explanation from the person who first held the context.

The former `Routes` and `Channels` fields are merged into `Paths`. The audit and
case stress test under TASK_CLAUDE_0028 could not distinguish them consistently:
the same observed episode repeatedly satisfied both fields. The merger is an
editorial and instrument-design decision based on that failed discriminant test.
It is not evidence that the resulting four-field tool is scientifically validated.

## Operational boundary

Each filled field is a testable claim about the continuation of one bounded
action. A nominal chat, title, calendar entry, or archive does not by itself count
as a functioning path, role, rhythm, or memory. The claim must be tied to an
observable use, and the design must state what would show that the field did not
work as expected.

The four fields are not necessary and sufficient conditions for community, a
maturity scale, or a universal score. Rituals, rules, value exchange, power,
privacy, resources, and participant costs remain design considerations that shape
the quality and ethics of every field.

## Manuscript and canon consequence

TASK_CODEX_0029 is authorized to synchronize Chapter 13, Chapter 15, the field
guide appendix, the evidence log, and `PALMA_METHOD_CANON_v1.1.md` to the
four-field form. Earlier five-field records remain unchanged as historical inputs.
