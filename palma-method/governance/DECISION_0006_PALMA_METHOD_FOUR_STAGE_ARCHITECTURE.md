# DECISION_0006 — Palma Method four-stage architecture

## Founder-directed decision

The canonical usable architecture of the Palma Method is:

1. **Diagnose** — establish what is actually missing, at the correct unit of
   analysis, using the Palma Relationship Map.
2. **Design** — choose a bounded intervention and specify the conditions that may
   support it, using the Palma Infrastructure Canvas.
3. **Launch** — run the intervention at real, bounded scale and observe Response
   Signals, treating Palma Moments as especially meaningful observable events.
4. **Transfer** — test what continues when the founder or organizer reduces direct
   initiation, using the Palma Handoff Test.

The stages form a cycle. A launch or transfer result may return the work to Diagnose
or Design. The order is usable and sequential enough to guide a practitioner, but it
does not promise a universal linear transformation.

## Categories that must remain separate

- **Stages:** Diagnose, Design, Launch, Transfer.
- **Cross-cutting stance:** Facilitate / Advise. It operates within every stage and
  is not a fifth sequential stage.
- **Object of diagnosis and strengthening:** Relationship Capital, under
  DECISION_0003. It is neither a stage nor a proprietary scientific discovery.
- **Observable transition event:** a Palma Moment — a significant observed
  counter-action that changes what happens next between two sides. It is not a
  stage, proof of durability, or a scientifically confirmed construct.
- **Tools:** Palma Relationship Map, Palma Infrastructure Canvas, Response Signals,
  Palma Handoff Test.
- **Outcomes:** context-specific changes in readiness to respond, participant-to-
  participant action, distributed relational infrastructure, and reduced dependence
  on the founder as the only route through which useful action can occur.

## Transfer boundary

Transfer does not mean removing the founder. It means reducing dependence on the
founder as the only route through which useful action can occur. A legitimate result
may show full continuation, partial continuation, continuing founder dependence that
is understood and accepted, or that transfer is neither needed nor wanted for the
system being examined.

The founder's continuing role must be named. A result is not a pass merely because
the founder disappeared, and a system is not a failure merely because the founder
remains important.

## Scientific status

Established sciences explain the components used by the Method. Palma's defensible
position is **conceptual novelty with untested propositions**. The narrow candidate
novelty is the proposed link between a consequential participant-initiated action at
dyadic level and distributed relational infrastructure at system level,
operationalized by whether continuation still requires the organizer to recreate it.

This decision does not claim scientific validation, proof, a validated scale, or
preliminary empirical confirmation. TCF, TAK.Shtab, and Bombom are illustrations and
hypothesis-generating cases, not controlled tests. Public language should describe
the Method as developed through real cases and field observations, not as tested or
validated through client work.

## Plain-language founder promise

> Palma Method helps a founder see which relationships can already produce a
> response, create conditions for people to act with one another, and move useful
> action from the founder's personal improvisation into routes that do not require
> the founder to push every time.

## Exact effect on DECISION_0005

This decision supersedes the following parts of DECISION_0005, and only these parts:

1. The section **“Decision”** insofar as it makes Orient, Diagnose, Decide, Design,
   Enable, and Evaluate the canonical six-phase core. Those six labels remain part of
   the historical reasoning track but cease to be the canonical public or operational
   architecture.
2. The section **“Relationship to Part IV”** insofar as it maps Chapter 20/Advise to
   Evaluate and treats Chapter 21/Handover as a non-stage principle. The current
   mapping is Chapter 17/Diagnose, Chapter 18/Design, Chapter 19/Launch, Chapter 20 as
   the Facilitate/Advise cross-cutting interlude, and Chapter 21/Transfer.
3. The section **“Superseded and preserved language”** insofar as it says
   `Diagnose → Design → Launch → Advise → Handover` is merely a client-engagement
   route rather than the Method core. Under this decision, the normalized four-stage
   form `Diagnose → Design → Launch → Transfer` is the Method core, and Advise /
   Facilitate is cross-cutting.
4. Any downstream statement whose authority rests only on DECISION_0005 and says
   Handover/Transfer is not a stage or says Advise/Evaluate is the fourth sequential
   stage.

The following parts of DECISION_0005 remain in force:

- the architecture is iterative rather than a guaranteed transformation;
- the prior Notion sequence `Diagnose → Audit → Align → Activate → Learn` is not
  canonical;
- DECISION_0003 remains authoritative and no competing Relationship Capital
  definition or sixth dimension may be introduced;
- field application is not evidence that the Method works until observations are
  assessed;
- the private Sempre Fixe material remains restricted and excluded unless separately
  authorized;
- the 2026-07-19 authorization record remains an immutable historical record.
