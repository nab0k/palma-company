# Palma Method — Canon v1.1

## Lineage and authority

This is the append-only successor to `PALMA_METHOD_CANON_v1.0.md`. Canon v1.0
remains unchanged as the historical reconciliation of DECISION_0003 and
DECISION_0005. This version carries forward everything in v1.0 except the specific
six-phase and Part IV mappings superseded by DECISION_0006.

Authority order for the current Method is:

1. DECISION_0003 for Relationship Capital.
2. DECISION_0006 for the four-stage Method architecture and the current effect on
   DECISION_0005.
3. The portions of DECISION_0005 explicitly preserved by DECISION_0006.
4. This operational canon.

## What Palma is

Palma Method is a practitioner framework for diagnosing whether reach has become
readiness to respond, designing conditions for people to create value with one
another, launching bounded interventions, and testing what continues without the
founder having to restart every useful action personally.

It draws on established research in social capital, relational capital, network
theory, interaction ritual theory, communities of practice, institutionalization,
and process consultation. Those fields explain the components. Palma selects,
connects, and operationalizes them as a practical cycle; the cycle and its tools have
not been independently validated.

## Founder promise

> Palma Method helps a founder see which relationships can already produce a
> response, create conditions for people to act with one another, and move useful
> action from the founder's personal improvisation into routes that do not require
> the founder to push every time.

This promise does not guarantee community, founder independence, or a positive
business outcome. It promises a disciplined way to see, decide, try, observe, and
test.

## Canonical architecture

### Stage 1 — Diagnose

Question: what is actually missing, at what unit of analysis, and what evidence
would show that diagnosis is wrong?

Required result: a specific problem, fixed unit of analysis, observable bottleneck,
alternative explanation, and falsifying observation.

Tool: **Palma Relationship Map**.

### Stage 2 — Design

Question: what should exist instead, and what is the smallest worthwhile
intervention?

Required result: an `if X, then Y` hypothesis, a minimum intervention, anticipated
success and failure signals, ethical limits, and any genuinely necessary boundary.

Tool: **Palma Infrastructure Canvas** — routes, roles, rhythms, channels, memory.
These five fields are a narrow decision sheet. They do not erase Chapter 13's wider
design vocabulary of channels, roles, rhythms, rituals, rules, and value exchange.
Rituals, rules, and value exchange shape how the five fields work; memory records
what the system can carry without fresh explanation.

### Stage 3 — Launch

Question: what appears only in real action, and what behavior gives a basis to keep,
change, stop, or redesign the intervention?

Required result: a real bounded test, a behavioral record rather than an impression,
and a next decision. A null result is information.

Tool: **Response Signals** — responds, returns, helps, connects, coordinates, takes
responsibility. They are a nonlinear observational checklist, not a maturity ladder
or score.

A **Palma Moment** is an especially meaningful observed event within Launch and
elsewhere in the cycle: a significant counter-action that changes what happens next
between two sides. It may follow an invitation. It is neither any response nor proof
that the relationship will endure.

Direction matters. A participant acting toward the founder may be important evidence
about that dyad, but it does not directly show distributed peer infrastructure. For
the proposed Transition Model, the load-bearing event is participant-to-participant
action. A possible contact path, a list of people someone says they could contact, or
stated willingness is not yet a confirmed route of continuation. Confirmation
requires an actual instance of that route being used without the organizer creating
the connection again.

### Stage 4 — Transfer

Question: what continues when the founder or organizer reduces direct initiation,
and is that continuation actually wanted?

Required result: an actually run Palma Handoff Test, an honest result category, and
a named continuing founder role. The observation window follows the system's natural
rhythm and is not universal.

Tool: **Palma Handoff Test**.

Transfer means reduced dependence on the founder as the only route for useful
action. It does not mean deleting the founder from the system. Full continuation,
partial continuation, accepted center-dependence, and no transfer needed are all
possible honest results. A passed test says only what continued during the observed
window.

## Cross-cutting stance — Facilitate / Advise

At every stage ask: am I increasing what this person or group can see and decide, or
am I deciding for them?

- Diagnose: ask before supplying the answer.
- Design: offer a testable bet that can be rejected or amended.
- Launch: do not rescue every wobble personally when the group's response is what
  needs observation.
- Transfer: name the founder's continuing role without exaggerating either necessity
  or independence.

Facilitate / Advise has no separate artifact or completion criterion and is not a
fifth stage.

## Relationship Capital

DECISION_0003 remains canonical. Relationship Capital is the value accumulated in
an organization's relationships that enables trust, cooperation, access, support,
and coordinated action. Its five dimensions are trust, reciprocity, access,
relationship strength, and network structure.

Relationship Capital is the object being diagnosed and strengthened. It is not a
stage, a synonym for personality or reputation, an owned asset, or a claimed
proprietary scientific discovery. The term is descriptive; **The Palma Method** is
the principal brand.

The Palma Relationship Map uses two layers without adding a sixth dimension:

- dyadic layer: trust, reciprocity, access, relationship strength;
- network layer: structure, bridges, redundancy, bottlenecks.

## Proposed transition and scientific status

Established research explains repeated encounters, mutual recognition, reciprocity,
network structure, institutionalization, and process consultation. Palma's narrow
new proposal links a consequential participant-initiated action at dyadic level to
distributed relational infrastructure at system level, observed through routes of
continuation that the organizer does not have to recreate.

The project's bounded targeted search did not find an equivalent integrated model;
this is not an exhaustive or worldwide-originality claim. Four of six newly checked
primary sources could not be verified in full text. Provan and Kenis's network-
governance typology is the closest organizer-independence neighbor found. Relational
event modeling is the closest event-level methodological neighbor, not an equivalent
substantive theory.

The defensible scientific position is **conceptual novelty with untested
propositions**. No Palma tool is a validated scale, psychometric instrument, tested
causal model, or proof of effectiveness. No fixed transition probability or universal
handoff window is known.

TCF, TAK.Shtab, and Bombom are illustrations and hypothesis-generating cases. They
are not controlled tests. TAK.Shtab's public record corroborates specified 2024
institutional features; it does not show that a formal Handoff Test was conducted.
The TCF evidence update is a five-case, one-coder exploratory check with a selected
contrast case, selection bias, and no comparison group. Its qualitative pattern is
consistent with the basic model and does not confirm it. It also exposed a construct
problem: in two cases, continued peer relationship outside scheduled training could
not be cleanly distinguished from ordinary gym-adjacent contact. P6 and P7 remain
wholly untested.
Public wording may say the Method was developed through real cases and field
observations. It must not say the Method was scientifically validated, proven, or
tested through client work.

## Outcomes and limits

The Method may support clearer diagnosis, more direct participant-to-participant
action, more distributed routes, or reduced single-person dependence. It does not
promise community, trust, belonging, permanence, or commercial success. A bounded
intervention may reveal that the original goal was unnecessary, unwanted, wrongly
scoped, or unsupported by the available relationships.

Stop rules, ethical limits, de-escalation, privacy restrictions, the decision owner's
authority, and the distinction between contribution and attribution carry forward
from Canon v1.0 and its underlying working materials.

## Open research questions

- measurement reliability and discriminant validity of the five dimensions;
- inter-rater reliability for identifying a Palma Moment;
- whether Response Signals behave independently or form an empirical sequence;
- whether participant-to-participant action predicts reduced organizer dependence;
- whether possible routes become confirmed through actual organizer-independent use;
- how to distinguish continued peer relationships from ordinary contact adjacent to
  a shared setting;
- P6 route-forming quality and P7 infrastructure mediation, both wholly untested;
- which system-level and contextual variables explain failed transfer;
- the remaining full-text, community-organizing, and stakeholder-engagement
  prior-art gaps recorded in the research package.
