# TASK_CODEX_0038 — Anonymous TCF participant source card

## Authorization

On 23 August 2026 Serhii Nabok directly instructed Codex to create a source card
for the completed interview and confirmed that the participant's name would not
be used in the book. He asked for a practical coded identity for internal work.

## Objective

Create one restricted, privacy-safe derivative card under the stable respondent
code `TCF-P10`, preserving useful evidence and contrary material without placing
the participant's identity, contact details, raw transcript locator, or exact
words in GitHub.

## Allowed changes

- Create a restricted source card for `TCF-P10`.
- Use the readable descriptor `established TCF participant` alongside the stable code.
- Separate participant reports, project interpretation, rival explanations, and verification gaps.
- Update generated research-package validation reports if the validator changes them.

## Forbidden changes

- Do not record a real name, initial, email, identifying URL, or named personal contacts.
- Do not copy the raw transcript or Notion-generated summary into GitHub.
- Do not retain or publish direct quotations.
- Do not present estimated network size, hypothetical founder absence, or social mechanisms as verified facts.
- Do not edit Notion, the manuscript, accepted decisions, corpus register, coding matrix, or synthesis.
- Do not treat this field account as proof of the Palma Method.

## Acceptance criteria

1. The card uses only `TCF-P10` and a non-identifying descriptive label.
2. Consent conditions and the requirement for participant review of every proposed book use are explicit.
3. The card distinguishes observed or reported behavior from interpretation and unsupported generalization.
4. Sensitive boundary material remains restricted and non-identifying.
5. Relevant validators pass or pre-existing failures are reported without concealment.
