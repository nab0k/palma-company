# TASK_CODEX_0030 — Clarify nonlinearity and community levels

## Objective

Create an append-only v0.6 review build that removes any remaining suggestion that
Palma's conceptual movement is a universal ladder. Clarify that relationships,
networks, communities, infrastructure, and collective action are different units or
properties that may coexist, appear in another order, or be unnecessary for the
founder's actual task.

## Allowed changes

- Copy v0.5 to v0.6 without altering v0.5.
- Make only local editorial repairs in the introduction, Chapter 8, conclusion,
  and scientific-status appendix.
- Reuse already supported distinctions and existing evidence.

## Forbidden changes

- Do not add claims or cases from the Classic report before source-card intake and
  verification.
- Do not alter the four-stage Method or four-field Canvas.
- Do not select international cases, edit Canon or approved Decisions, synchronize
  external documents, merge branches, or open a pull request.

## Acceptance criteria

1. The conceptual movement is explicitly described as a map of possible
   transformations rather than a universal sequence.
2. Community is not presented as a mandatory stage, superior category, or synonym
   for distributed infrastructure.
3. No unsupported factual or causal claim is introduced.
4. v0.5 remains byte-for-byte unchanged.
