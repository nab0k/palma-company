# Written/Conversational Questionnaire — Low-Regularity Member

Format: informal conversation or written answers
Status: draft
Participant status: current TCF member, ~6 months, one weekly group class plus ~2 weekly solo "open gym" sessions
Related sources: SC-099 (this case's intake card); SC-095/096/097/098 (positively-connected TCF member interviews, for contrast)

## Why this interview matters

Every TCF account so far comes from a member who formed real connections at the
gym. This is the first case of someone who formed none, despite attending for
six months and already knowing several people there before joining. The first,
informal pass at why (attendance regularity) was worked out jointly with the
author, not volunteered independently — this follow-up exists to test that
explanation properly, including the rival explanation it hasn't yet ruled out.

## Questions

1. Outside of TCF — in other group settings you've been part of (past jobs,
   classes, social groups) — do you generally find it easy to strike up
   connections with people, or does this same pattern show up elsewhere too?

2. Think about open-gym sessions specifically. Is there truly zero
   acknowledgment from other people there — no eye contact, no nod, nothing —
   or is there some minimal surface-level recognition that just never turns
   into an actual relationship?

3. Of the people you already knew before joining TCF — do you actually
   interact with them *at* the gym, or does that relationship stay separate
   from your gym time even though you're both physically there?

4. How would you describe your interaction with Miguel? Does it feel personal,
   or is it more the ordinary contact any coach has with any member —
   regardless of how connected that member is to others?

5. Do you actually want more connection with people at TCF, or is showing up,
   training, and leaving exactly what you want from this particular place?

6. If you imagine going to the group class three times a week instead of
   once — do you think that alone would change things? Or do you suspect
   something else is really going on?

7. Is there anything about your experience at TCF I haven't asked about that
   you think matters here?
