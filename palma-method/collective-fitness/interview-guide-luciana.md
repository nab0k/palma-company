# Interview Guide — Luciana (member)

Duration: approximately 30–40 minutes
Format: semi-structured
Status: draft
Participant status: current TCF member, originally from Brazil
Related sources: SC-043, SC-044 (Miguel interviews); interview-guide-members.md
(base template)

## Why this interview matters

Every account of TCF so far is the founder's. Luciana is the first
participant perspective, and she is a specific kind of participant: she
became personally close to Miguel and Margarida rather than only attending
classes. That makes her useful for two different things at once — an
ordinary member's account of what TCF is like, and a close-up view of how a
founder-participant relationship actually forms, which is the same question
the Founder's own TCF story (SC-046) raises from the inside.

## Opening — 0–5 minutes

- Confirm purpose, consent, recording preference, and confidentiality.
- Ask Luciana to describe TCF in her own words, before introducing any Palma
  terminology.

## Arrival and return — 5–12 minutes

1. How did you first hear about TCF, and what made you try it?
2. What made you come back a second time, a third time?
3. Having moved from Brazil, did TCF feel different from other gyms or
   groups you'd been part of before? What specifically was different?

## How the relationship with Miguel and Margarida formed — 12–24 minutes

4. Tell me about how you actually met Miguel and Margarida — was it during a
   class, before, or after?
5. Do you remember the first real conversation you had with either of them?
   What was it about?
6. How did that turn into something more than a friendly hello — was there a
   specific moment, or did it build gradually over repeated visits?
7. Do you see Margarida or Miguel outside TCF now? Do you do anything
   together that has nothing to do with training?

## Beyond the founders — 24–30 minutes

8. Are there other members you know by name and talk to regularly? Roughly
   how many?
9. Is there a ritual or habit at TCF — something that repeats every class or
   every week — that helped people get to know each other?
10. Have you helped another member with something unrelated to fitness, or
    has someone helped you?

## Edges and limits — 30–35 minutes

11. Are there people at TCF who stay on the outside, who never really
    connect with others? What's different about the people who become
    "regulars" versus the ones who stay strangers?

## Closing test — 35–40 minutes

12. If TCF closed tomorrow, what would you actually lose — the classes
    themselves, or these specific people?
13. What have I not asked that matters?
14. Confirm permissions: may your name and this account be used, in what
    form, and are there parts that must stay off the record?

## Interviewer reminders

- Ask for concrete episodes, not general impressions.
- Do not let the Miguel/Margarida friendship crowd out the ordinary-member
  questions — both are needed; this is a member account first, a close-up
  second.
- The test this interview exists to run: question 12 is the book's Chapter 2
  test ("if the organization disappeared tomorrow") asked directly of a
  participant rather than inferred from the founder's account.
- Avoid collecting health or other sensitive personal information unless
  essential and explicitly consented.
- Confirm permission separately for any exact quote or identifiable story,
  and separately for any detail involving Miguel or Margarida personally.
