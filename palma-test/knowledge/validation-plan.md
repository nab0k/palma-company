# Validation plan

## Claim we can make now

“Palma Test is a theory-informed qualitative diagnostic that organises evidence through six questions.”

## Claim we cannot make yet

“Palma Test is a scientifically validated test” or “a strong/weak result predicts commercial performance.”

## Phase 1 — construct and content validity

1. Complete a structured literature review for each construct and competing explanations.
2. Define inclusion/exclusion rules and observable indicators in the Canon.
3. Ask 6–10 independent experts in sociology, community psychology, organisational behaviour, HCI and strategic communication to judge relevance, clarity and coverage.
4. Calculate item-level and scale-level content validity indices; document disagreements rather than averaging them away.

Exit criterion: every lens has a defensible construct definition, no major missing construct remains, and expert agreement meets a predeclared threshold.

## Phase 2 — cognitive interviewing

Run 15–25 think-aloud interviews across owners, advisers, community operators and people who participate in those relationships. Test whether each question elicits the intended construct rather than status, popularity or aspiration.

Exit criterion: users can distinguish evidence from guesses and interpret the six questions consistently enough to revise wording.

## Phase 3 — qualitative reliability

Build 40–60 anonymised case vignettes with evidence packets. Two or more trained raters classify evidence states independently. Measure agreement with weighted kappa or Krippendorff’s alpha, report disagreements by lens, and revise the rubric.

The LLM must be evaluated against the same gold cases. Track unsupported-claim rate, evidence-trace accuracy, relationship-type agreement and decision usefulness separately.

## Phase 4 — construct validity

Collect a larger sample and compare Palma lenses with established measures where constructs overlap, without assuming equivalence. Pre-register convergent and discriminant hypotheses. Use exploratory analysis before any confirmatory factor model because Palma began as a qualitative method, not a scale.

## Phase 5 — criterion and longitudinal validity

Define outcomes before observing them: repeat participation, introductions, member-led action, recovery after disagreement, decision implementation and relevant commercial/advocacy outcomes. Test whether baseline evidence states predict changes over 3–12 months. Do not use sales as a universal criterion.

## Product-level evaluation

- Evidence traceability: every claim links to submitted evidence or an explicit unknown.
- Calibration: confidence falls when evidence is sparse or conflicting.
- Stability: materially similar inputs yield materially similar readings.
- Counterfactual tests: changing only an irrelevant detail must not change the decision.
- Bias audit: names, geography, company prestige and writing fluency must not inflate a reading.
- Human usefulness: owners can name a clearer decision after the reading, measured before/after and again after 30 days.

## Governance

Version the Canon, prompt, model and gold set separately. Any material change triggers regression tests. Keep a decision log explaining why a construct or rule changed. Publish limitations and negative results.
