# Palma Test knowledge system

This directory separates the method from the model. The language model is an interpreter; it is not the source of truth.

- `palma-canon.md` defines the six constructs and the evidence rules the app may use.
- `evidence-map.md` records where each construct comes from and how far the source can support it.
- `validation-plan.md` describes how Palma Test can move from scientifically grounded to empirically validated.
- `rag-architecture.md` defines what belongs in retrieval and what must remain deterministic code.

## Epistemic status

Palma Test 0.1 is a theory-informed qualitative diagnostic. It is not yet a validated psychometric instrument. The app must not claim that its six lenses, relationship classification or recommendations have been scientifically validated until the studies in `validation-plan.md` have been completed and published with their limitations.

HBR and practitioner books can inform managerial relevance, examples, decision framing and writing. They cannot establish construct validity on their own. Peer-reviewed sociology, community psychology, organisational behaviour and HCI form the evidence layer; Palma cases and expert judgment form the practice layer.
