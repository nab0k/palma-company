## Source hierarchy

1. Peer-reviewed empirical studies and validated instruments.
2. Peer-reviewed theory and scholarly books.
3. Replications, systematic reviews and cross-cultural validation studies.
4. HBR and credible practitioner research for managerial application.
5. Palma cases and expert practice for hypothesis generation.
6. Writing books for voice and compression only.

The four writing/purpose books already used in Palma work belong to layers 4–6. They can improve framing and prose; they must not be cited as validation of a construct.
